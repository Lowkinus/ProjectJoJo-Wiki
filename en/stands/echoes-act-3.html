Skill 1 — BARRAGE → THREE FREEZE

4.7s cooldown2.8 tiles14 / 23 hits

v237 raised base Barrage damage per hit from 0.026 to 0.060. Full charge takes 1.65s for 23 hits. When the Barrage ends, the last real target it damaged is queued for THREE FREEZE automatically if the target is still valid.

Skill 2 — THREE FREEZE

9s cooldown9 tiles9s Freeze

Selects a single target through the current First-Contact system and freezes it for nine seconds. It can also freeze a vehicle for nine seconds when the line contacts the vehicle instead.

TARGET RULE

One main targetNew target releases oldBreaks beyond 9 tiles

The owner-target link cares about range and floor. If the target dies, leaves the valid range, or the Stand state becomes invalid, the Freeze/Mark cleans itself up.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Freeze the dangerous one

Do not waste control on the zombie at the back doing nothing. Freeze the bite threat, the blocker at the doorway, or the target your teammate needs to survive.

Barrage is a CC combo

When the threat is already close, Skill 1 does the work for you: Barrage, then automatic THREE FREEZE on the last real target. No panic double-press required.

Skill 2 opens from range

If walking into 2.8 tiles is a bad idea, use the direct 9-tile Freeze first and handle the rest of the crowd while the priority target is parked.

Yes, vehicles too

THREE FREEZE can stop vehicles for nine seconds. In MP that can be control, rescue, or pure “please stop making this situation worse.”

Keep this in mind: Echoes wins by choosing the right target. If every Freeze goes into the first harmless body you see, the Stand cannot make the important decision for you.
