HARVEST

HARVEST has one main resource: a shared colony of up to 500 units. Shot, Swarm, Storm and Search all compete for that same pool.

HOW TO GET HARVEST

Enable Project JoJo Core and the HARVEST addon, then use a normal Stand Arrow. HARVEST joins the shared Stand Pool and appears in Super Arrow selection while the addon is enabled.

CONTROLS

CAPS LOCK — Summon / Dismiss
1 — HARVEST SHOT
2 — NEEDLE SWARM
3 — HARVEST SEARCH COMMAND
4 — HARVEST STORM

Combat follows the Core Targeting Mode. Search Command is gameplay UI and uses pointer interaction to choose templates, quantities and crew.

COLONY — 0 / 500

Combat and Search draw from one shared colony of up to 500 Harvest. Units sent away on Search are reserved, lowering the regeneration ceiling until that job returns or is recalled.

  • Still: 0.80 / sec after the grace period
  • Seated / Vehicle: 4.05 / sec
  • Rest: 9.00 / sec
  • Sleep: 14.40 / sec

Walking/running is not a normal recovery state. The HARVEST regeneration Sandbox option scales colony recovery; it does not directly accelerate Search.

Example: Send 150 units to Search and your available recovery ceiling is roughly 350 until those 150 return. The system does not regenerate a second free copy of the crew.

SKILL 1 — HARVEST SHOT

Costs 1 Harvest. Send a single unit as fast ranged harassment with light damage and mini knockback/CC. Hold to continue sending one at a time.

Supported Barrage Guard can defend against a Shot from the front.

SKILL 2 — NEEDLE SWARM

Minimum commitment: 7 Harvest. Hold to add more units. The selected group attaches to one target, waits, then strikes together. Larger commitments increase the payoff.

If the target dies/disappears or breaks the valid leash before resolution, the pending group is cancelled and reserved units are refunded by the system.

SKILL 3 — HARVEST SEARCH COMMAND

Open Search Command and choose a real InventoryItem already in your inventory as the template, then choose requested quantity and assigned crew.

Template handling

Search is not limited to medicine. It can inspect nested bags and tries to keep important state separate: for example full/half/empty fluid containers, condition/used-state differences and relevant weapon state instead of grouping only by FullType.

For modded/runtime items, authority preflights whether the requested item can be safely rebuilt before START SEARCH reserves the crew. Unsupported recreation should give a compatibility message rather than consuming the team first and failing later.

Quantity / Crew

  • Quantity: x1–x500
  • x1–x20 = minimum 20 Harvest
  • x21–x500 = minimum crew equals requested quantity
  • More than the minimum crew speeds the Search work
  • Total batch weight adds transport time

HARVEST Stand Level can reduce the authored Search-work portion through progression; outbound travel, return travel and delivery do not disappear into instant teleportation.

ACTIVE JOBS / RECALL

Multiple jobs can exist independently. The UI shows job phase/ETA, and RECALL cancels only that job, immediately releases its reserved Harvest and discards items that were never delivered.

Search audio is keyed per job and follows the actual job position, so simultaneous jobs can play correctly. Personal Project JoJo Volume also applies.

SKILL 4 — HARVEST STORM

Minimum commitment: 33 Harvest. Commit part of the colony around yourself for repeated area pressure, mini knockback and crowd interruption. Larger commitments last longer up to the authored cap.

Barrage Guard checks guarding players individually: a successful guard can stop that player's Storm hit without deleting the Storm for everybody else.

HOW TO MANAGE THE COLONY

Do not treat 500 as ammunition you should always empty. It is one budget split between surviving this fight and sending workers away for future resources.

Example: If 300 units are away on Search, only 200 remain for combat. Spending most of those on a huge Storm can leave almost nothing for Shot or Needle Swarm until jobs return or are recalled.

CLIENT / SANDBOX OPTIONS

HARVEST follows the Core Normal / Minimal / Hidden HUD, HUD Opacity/Scale, Personal Volume and Targeting Mode. Search Command remains available because it is gameplay UI.

The addon also exposes Harvest Regeneration Speed to scale colony recovery without turning it into Search-speed acceleration or bypassing the reservation ceiling.

Simple loop: check remaining Colony → decide how much the current fight deserves → remember that every Search crew disappears from your combat budget until it comes home.
