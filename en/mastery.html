STAND MASTERY / BALANCE

Low-level Stands are intentionally weak. High mastery now rewards both damage and skill fluency instead of only increasing raw damage.

Current rules

LV.1 = 50% Damage with authored base cooldowns • LV.100 = 120% Damage plus skill-specific cooldown mastery. Basic Punch cooldowns do not shrink so animation/contact cadence stays safe.

LV.1 Damage50%
LV.100 Damage120%
LV.1 → 100≈ 1,059 kills
EXPStand kill only

New progression curve

A zombie actually killed by the Stand grants 1 EXP. Level costs start at 5 EXP and step upward every 8 levels. The early game remains intentionally weak, but reaching mastery no longer requires the old ~1,671 Stand kills.

Approximate milestones: LV.5 = 20 • LV.10 = 46 • LV.20 = 109 • LV.25 = 144 • LV.30 = 184 • LV.50 = 371 • LV.75 = 676 • LV.90 = 896 • LV.100 = 1,059 Stand kills.

Why do some skills receive larger reductions?

The Hand, Magician's Red, Crazy Diamond and Harvest already pay Gauge/resource costs, so they should not be double-taxed by long cooldowns. Temporal invulnerability/control is balanced by uptime, while THREE FREEZE keeps its full 9-second cooldown because the control itself lasts 9 seconds.
