SKILLS & MECHANICS

Skill 1 — PUNCH

CD 0.4s · Range 2.9

Punish from a safe angle. The 77% critical chance applies only to individual targets actually facing away from the attacker.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

CD 5.3s · Range 3.15 · 17 hits

Close pressure that participates in supported Barrage Clash and Guard rules. Back-facing critical checks are per victim, not a global buff.

Skill 3 — EPITAPH COUNTER

CD 12s · 5s base window

Wait for a committed incoming attack, then reposition away from the attacker or behind them. The test-patch protection work covers overlapping hits only during the active counter/teleport window.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / TIME ERASE

CD 32s · Tap Dash up to 13.75 tiles · Hold Erase 10s

Tap to dash, or hold for Time Erase. Leave Time Erase early by pressing skill 4 again. During Erase, normal Stand skills are locked.

BACKSTAB CRITICALS & HUD

Base crit is 15%. Attacking a target whose back is exposed increases that target’s crit chance to 77%. The HUD tries to display 77% when a back-facing target is selected; the actual damage roll is calculated independently for each target. This does not give King Crimson a constant 77% crit buff.

Example: two zombies stand in front of you; one faces you and the other faces away. Only the second is eligible for the rear-hit chance.
