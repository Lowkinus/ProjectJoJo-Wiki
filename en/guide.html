PROJECT JOJO — COMPLETE STAND GUIDE

Guide version: v0.23.0.232
Project Zomboid Build 42.20+ • SP / Host / MP

This update brings the Wiki in line with the current mod behavior, especially systems that changed after the older guide: Glass Homing, SHA, THREE FREEZE, Rapier Shot and several attack-contact rules.

The goal is player-facing information: what a skill does, how to use it, its approximate timing/range, and what can interrupt it — without turning the guide into source-code documentation.

Getting Started↑ TOP

BASIC CONTROLS

CAPS LOCK — Summon / Dismiss Stand
1 / 2 / 3 / 4 — Stand skills on the normal top-row keys

Many skills have separate tap/partial and full-charge actions. If a skill seems to be missing part of its behavior, check whether that version requires a full hold.

Mouse direction remains the main attack direction. Close melee has some aim assistance, but you still need to face the right way.

Core Systems↑ TOP

ATTACK AIM & CONTACT

Many attacks now check the path they physically travel at the moment of impact.

  • Punch/Barrage: first body reached by the Stand path can be hit
  • Knife / Rapier Shot / Fireball / Glass Shot: a body in front can intercept the projectile
  • The Hand ERASE: contact is checked when the hand reaches the attack line; a real miss stays a miss
  • Walls, doors, floor differences and blocked paths still matter

In short: aim where the attack is actually going, not only at the name of the target you want.

Getting Started↑ TOP

STAND LEVEL SYSTEM

Each Stand stores its own Level from LV.1 → LV.100. LV.1 starts at roughly 50% of full Stand damage and scales toward full power at LV.100.

Only zombies actually killed by the Stand count for Stand EXP.

Using a Stand Arrow to roll a new Stand does not erase the old Stand's saved Level/EXP. If you get that Stand again later, its progress is still there.

Death resets progression for that character. Disabling Stand Level in Sandbox makes every Stand operate at full power while saved real progression remains stored.

Getting Started↑ TOP

STAND ARROW

A normal Stand Arrow has 3 successful uses and uses Project Zomboid's native Condition: 3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → removed.

Condition is spent only after a successful Stand roll, so a failed use does not randomly eat one of the three uses.

Default zombie drop chance is roughly 1 / 250. Tool/garage/mechanic storage is one of the better map-search sources, while vehicle loot is intentionally rarer.

With Infinite Stand Arrow enabled in Sandbox, the Arrow does not lose Condition, but you still need at least one Arrow in your inventory.

Core Systems↑ TOP

MULTIPLAYER / PVP RULES

Project JoJo separates HP damage from non-damaging utility/control.

PvP/Safety can block HP damage while utility such as Time Stop, THREE FREEZE, Space Pull, Epitaph reposition, First Bomb attachment and Barrage Clash can still function.

Important exceptions: SHA does not target PvP/Safety-protected players, The Hand ERASE cannot lethally delete protected players, and Time Erase cannot be targeted normally by Stand attacks.

Core Systems↑ TOP

BARRAGE CLASH

Supported offensive Barrages can clash when players are close enough — roughly 5.25 tiles — and are actually facing/aiming into each other.

During the Clash, Barrage hits are spent against each other before normal damage. Crazy Diamond Restore Rush is not an offensive Barrage Clash attack.

Core Systems↑ TOP

TIME STOP — IMPORTANT RULES

Star Platinum and The World share the main Time Stop system. Supported players, zombies, actors, vehicles, attacks and relevant timers are paused.

Damage dealt to supported frozen targets is held and released when time resumes.

SP/TW users can perceive stopped time differently, but perception alone does not grant movement. Fully charging your own Time Stop can create your own movement window inside another Time Stop.

Core Systems↑ TOP

TIME STOP VS TIME ERASE

A new full Time Erase cannot start while a Time Stop is already active or beginning.

If Time Erase was active first and a Time Stop arrives, the remaining durations are spent against each other:

  • Shorter Time Stop: the Stop is consumed and Erase continues
  • Longer Time Stop: Erase ends and the leftover Stop continues
  • Equal duration: both cancel each other
Stand Guides↑ TOP

STAR PLATINUM

ROLE:
Brawler / Crit Combo / Close Range / Time Control

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want aggressive melee, a growing Crit threat and a short but very strong Time Stop.

SKILL 1 — ORA PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.36 sec • Range: about 2.75 tiles • Full charge: about 1.2 sec.

Star Platinum is currently the only Stand with the active 5-hit Punch combo. Continue punching within about 0.95 sec to advance the chain; hit 5 is the strongest.

Tap for pressure. Charge when you have room to commit to a heavier hit.

CRITICAL SYSTEM

Starts at 15%. Every real Punch/Barrage contact that does not Crit adds +1%, up to 50%.

A Crit deals x3.5 damage and resets the chance to 15%. Star Finger can use the stored chance, but a failed Finger does not build it.

SKILL 2 — ORA BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5 sec • Normal: 15 hits • Full charge: 25 hits with increased damage.

Barrage builds Crit on non-Crit contacts and can enter Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 — STAR FINGER

Cooldown: about 7 sec • Range: about 5 tiles • Full charge: about 1.05 sec.

A piercing line attack. Multiple targets can be hit if they are physically lined up in the Finger path. It can also use Star Platinum's stored Crit chance.

SKILL 4 — TIME DASH / TIME STOP

Cooldown: about 35 sec.

Tap: collision-aware dash up to about 4.5 tiles.
Full charge: charge for about 1 sec, then a 1.7 sec wind-up before a 5 sec Time Stop.

Damage dealt to supported frozen victims is held and released when time resumes. SP can also fully charge its own Time Stop from inside another Time Stop to gain its own movement window.

THE WORLD

ROLE:
Heavy Burst / Range / Time Duelist

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want straightforward heavy pressure, a real ranged projectile and the longest normal Time Stop.

SKILL 1 — MUDA PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.39 sec • Range: about 2.8 tiles • Full charge: about 1.2 sec.

A heavy single-target punch. The World does not use Star Platinum's active 5-hit Punch combo.

SKILL 2 — MUDA BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5.2 sec • Normal: 15 hits • Full charge: 28 hits.

Strong close pressure and a supported Barrage Clash attack.

SKILL 3 — KNIFE THROW

Cooldown: about 1.6 sec • Range: about 9 tiles • Full charge: about 0.9 sec.

The knife travels along the direction you actually aim. A body in front can intercept it, and a real miss stays a miss.

SKILL 4 — TIME DASH / ZA WARUDO

Cooldown: about 42 sec.

Tap: collision-aware dash up to about 5.25 tiles.
Full charge: about 1 sec charge + 0.75 sec wind-up, then 9 sec Time Stop.

The World can fully charge its own stop while frozen by another Time Stop, gaining its own movement window. If the original stop still has time left afterward, it can freeze you again.

SILVER CHARIOT

ROLE:
Hit & Run / Speed Gauge / Precision

Difficulty:
Medium–High

Best For:
Players who like constant movement, short attack windows and resource management built around speed.

PASSIVE — MOVEMENT + SPEED GAUGE

While summoned, Silver Chariot moves at about 1.12x speed.

Speed Gauge max: 100. Regen depends on movement:

  • Idle: +0.1/sec
  • Walking: +1/sec
  • Running/Jogging: +11/sec
  • Sprinting: +20/sec

Gauge regen pauses during external Time Stop or THREE FREEZE. Movement is part of the kit, not just positioning.

SKILL 1 — STAB

Cooldown: about 0.22 sec • Range: about 3.1 tiles • Full charge: about 1.1 sec.

Fast single-target pressure with no Speed Gauge cost.

SKILL 2 — RAPIER RUSH

Cooldown: about 3 sec • Cost: 18 Gauge • Normal: 18 hits • Full charge: 26 hits.

Your main close-range burst. Move to refill, spend it, then reposition.

SKILL 3 — CIRCLE SWEEP

Cooldown: about 3.5 sec • Cost: 25 Gauge • Sweep reach: about 4.2 tiles.

Full charge increases damage/impact, not the basic sweep footprint. Best when several targets are already around you.

SKILL 4 — RAPIER SHOT

Cooldown: about 5.2 sec • Range: about 8.2 tiles • Full charge: about 1.05 sec.

The sword follows the real shot line and can be intercepted by another body.

After firing, the sword takes roughly 5.2 sec to return. Silver Chariot skills stay locked during that return window, so fire it when you have room to disengage.

KILLER QUEEN

ROLE:
Trap / AOE / Autonomous Pressure

Difficulty:
High

Best For:
Players who like traps, area control and using Sheer Heart Attack as a second source of pressure.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.39 sec • Range: about 2.7 tiles • Full charge: about 1.2 sec.

A basic close-range option for when enemies reach you before your bomb plan is ready.

SKILL 2 — BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5.2 sec • Normal: 16 hits • Full charge: 24 hits.

Useful for close pressure and Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 TAP — FIRST BOMB

Tap or partial charge to place First Bomb. Cooldown: about 0.7 sec • Range: about 5.5 tiles.

If you aim at a living target, the bomb attaches and follows that target. Otherwise it is placed at the world position. Press Skill 3 again while a bomb exists to retrieve/remove it.

Bomb attachment itself is utility, so it can still be attached to a PvP/Safety-protected player; protected players take no HP damage when it detonates.

SKILL 4 — DETONATE

Cooldown: about 6.8 sec • Base radius: 2.5 tiles • Full charge: about 1.25 sec, radius about 3.25 tiles plus more damage.

An attached target takes the primary hit while nearby targets take reduced splash. Ground bombs deal the most damage near the center and fall off toward the edge.

SKILL 3 FULL — SHEER HEART ATTACK

Full charge for about 0.9 sec to deploy or remotely recall SHA. This is separate from First Bomb's placement cooldown.

SHA now behaves as its own autonomous unit: it moves, selects targets and attacks without needing constant commands, with smoother movement than older versions.

  • Max HP: 150
  • Search range: ~28 tiles
  • Attack distance: ~2.65 tiles
  • Bomb wind-up: 1.5 sec
  • Attack cooldown: 4 sec
  • Explosion damage: 50% of KQ Detonate
  • Stored repair: +1 HP every 5 sec

There is no passive HP drain while SHA is deployed. HP is lost from incoming damage and from activity: every 2 real explosions cost 1 HP.

The two-explosion counter persists across manual recall/redeploy and resets after SHA is destroyed and fully repaired.

SHA can be damaged by supported Stand/weapon attacks, and The Hand ERASE can destroy it. At 0 HP, the owner's right hand is badly injured and SHA cannot be redeployed until fully repaired.

PvP/Safety: protected players are not selected as SHA targets; if protection changes during wind-up, SHA cancels that player target, and protected players are skipped by splash damage.

Zombie aggro: SHA chases its own targets, but it does not force nearby zombies to switch targets and swarm it. It can guard and pressure an area, but it is not a free horde lure.

ECHOES ACT 3

ROLE:
Single-Target CC / Team Setup

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want to stop the most dangerous target in the room rather than compete for raw DPS.

SKILL 1 — BARRAGE → THREE FREEZE

Cooldown: about 4.7 sec • Range: 2.8 tiles • Normal: 14 hits • Full charge: 23 hits.

The Barrage remembers the last real target it actually hit. When the Barrage ends, THREE FREEZE is automatically applied to that target if it is still valid.

That turns Skill 1 into a close-range combo that naturally ends in crowd control.

SKILL 2 — THREE FREEZE

Cooldown: about 9 sec • Range: about 9 tiles • Freeze duration: 9 sec.

Deals no direct damage. It can freeze a targeted living actor immediately, and targeted vehicles can also be frozen for 9 sec.

One Echoes owner controls one main target at a time. Freezing a new target releases the old one, and invalid/out-of-range/different-floor targets are cleared.

PvP/Safety protects HP damage, but does not automatically block this kind of non-damaging crowd control.

TEAM USE

Freeze the zombie about to bite a teammate, the player you need to stop, or a vehicle that needs to stay put.

Echoes is strongest when you use it to remove the most urgent threat from the fight for a few seconds.

THE HAND

ROLE:
Execute / Mobility / Rescue / Space Control

Difficulty:
High

Best For:
Players who like heavy short-range attacks mixed with movement, teammate rescue and ERASE, all sharing one resource.

SPACE GAUGE

Maximum 100 • Passive regen 2/sec.

  • Punch contact: +5
  • Barrage hit: +1
  • Space Pull: costs 20
  • Space Dash: costs 35
  • ERASE: costs 35

Some committed utility/erase actions can spend Gauge even if they do not end up hitting a target, so aim before you commit.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.48 sec • Range: 2.8 tiles • Full charge: about 1.25 sec.

Heavy close-range hit and the best single-contact source of Space Gauge.

SKILL 2 — BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5.4 sec • Normal: 16 hits • Full charge: 25 hits.

Each real contact can refill Gauge, making it useful for rebuilding resources in melee.

SKILL 3 TAP — SPACE DASH

Tap/partial charge to dash along the mouse direction, with a command range up to about 14 tiles. The path is checked for obstacles.

A successful dash costs 35 Gauge. If movement is completely blocked and no dash happens, the movement cost is not spent.

SKILL 3 FULL — SPACE PULL

Full charge threshold: about 0.7 sec • Cost: 20 Gauge • Pull range: about 21.875 tiles along the forward corridor.

Players can be pulled even when PvP/Safety protects HP damage, because Pull is utility and does not deal player HP damage.

Use it to rescue teammates, bring a target into range, or reorganize a bad fight.

SKILL 4 — ERASE

Cooldown: about 25 sec • Range: about 3.25 tiles • Cost: 35 Gauge.

ERASE is a close-range attack. Contact is checked when the hand actually reaches the attack line, so a real miss stays a miss.

It works on supported living/world targets and SHA. PvP/Safety-protected players cannot be lethally erased.

MAGICIAN'S RED

ROLE:
Ranged / Fire / Horde Control / Utility

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want ranged pressure, area denial and a real resource to manage.

FIRE GAUGE

Maximum 100 • Regen 2/sec, so 0 → 100 takes about 50 sec.

  • Fireball: 8
  • Flame Spray: 20
  • Fire Control: free
  • Cross Fire: 32 base / about 55 at full charge

SKILL 1 — FIREBALL

Cooldown: about 1.7 sec • Range: 8 tiles • Full charge: about 1.05 sec.

The projectile follows the real aim line, so a body in front can intercept it.

It can still ignite supported utilities. A fully charged Fireball that does not hit a living target or utility can create ground fire when the separate 9 sec ground-fire cooldown is ready.

SKILL 2 — FLAME SPRAY

Cooldown: about 5.2 sec • Range: 5.2 tiles • Normal: 22 hits • Full charge: 32 hits.

Sustained fire pressure that can ignite supported non-player living targets. Strong in choke points; risky around anything you care about that also burns.

SKILL 3 — FIRE CONTROL

Cooldown: about 4.4 sec • Base radius: 3.2 tiles • Full charge radius: about 4.32 tiles.

No direct damage and no Fire Gauge cost. Extinguishes nearby world fire and burning supported actors, including players.

SKILL 4 — CROSS FIRE BOMB

Cooldown: about 12 sec • Range: 8 tiles • Base radius: 3.2 tiles • Full charge: about 1.45 sec, radius up to roughly 5.92 tiles.

Your main AOE burst. It creates fire at the impact area; Fire Gauge cost starts at 32 and rises with charge to roughly 55 at full power.

CRAZY DIAMOND

ROLE:
Support Bruiser / Restore / Guided Glass

Difficulty:
High

Best For:
Players who want to switch between combat and restoration, with a marked-target projectile as part of the loop.

RESTORE GAUGE

Maximum 100 • Passive refill is only 0.01/sec, or roughly 1 point every 100 sec.

The main refill comes from real offensive contacts: Punch +0.5, Barrage hit +0.15625, Glass hit +0.625.

Main costs: Restore Punch 6.25, Restore Rush 1.25 per successful heal tick, Glass Homing 6.25.

Because passive regen is so slow, Crazy Diamond is built around fighting to earn Restore Gauge, then spending it to support the team.

SKILL 1 — DORAAA / RESTORE PUNCH

Attack Mode: ~0.37 sec cooldown, ~2.8 tile range, full charge ~1.2 sec.

Restore Mode: restores on the real punch contact. Base heal is about 18; full charge about 36. Gauge is spent only on a successful Restore contact.

It can restore other supported living targets or supported world targets under the hit. Crazy Diamond does not use this as self-healing.

SKILL 2 — DORA RUSH / RESTORE RUSH

Attack Mode: ~5 sec cooldown, 17 hits; full charge ~1.7 sec → 25 hits.

Restore Mode: about 4 heal per successful tick, rising to about 8.4 at full charge. Each successful heal tick costs 1.25 Gauge.

SKILL 3 — ATTACK / RESTORE MODE

Very short mode-toggle cooldown: about 0.25 sec. It changes the behavior of Skills 1, 2 and 4.

Attack Mode is for damage. Restore Mode changes the kit toward healing/repair and enables Glass Homing.

SKILL 4 ATTACK — GLASS SHOT + MARK

Cooldown: about 5.2 sec • Range: 9 tiles • Full charge: about 1.1 sec.

The shard travels straight along the mouse direction. The living target actually hit becomes the current Glass Mark.

If the shard reaches the world instead, the impact point becomes the mark. Only one mark can exist at a time.

The mark lasts 60 sec. A marked living target dying invalidates that actor mark, and Homing needs the mark to remain on roughly the same floor.

SKILL 4 RESTORE — GLASS HOMING

Requires a mark. With no mark, the skill is rejected before cooldown or Gauge is spent.

Cooldown: about 7 sec • Restore Gauge cost: 6.25 for a valid cast.

The shard launches in the raw mouse direction first, then curves back toward the marked target or point at about 7.15 tiles/sec.

It physically travels the route: walls can stop it, and another living body can intercept it before it reaches the marked target.

A successful Skill 4 cast refreshes the mark to 60 sec.

Glass Homing is still an offensive guided projectile. Restore Mode is what lets the broken glass return toward the mark; it is not a healing missile.

WHAT CAN RESTORE FIX?

Besides supported living wounds/status, Restore can repair many supported things: vehicle parts, item condition, aged/rotten/burnt food, windows/broken glass, doors, barricades, trees, thumpables and other supported world-health objects.

Crazy Diamond is not only a healer; a large part of the kit is restoring damaged objects and world state as well.

KING CRIMSON

ROLE:
Counter / Survival / Escape / Temporal Control

Difficulty:
High

Best For:
Players who prefer reading the next dangerous moment, countering it, then using Time Erase to escape or reposition.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.4 sec • Range: 2.9 tiles • Full charge: about 1.2 sec.

Straightforward heavy melee for when you need direct pressure.

SKILL 2 — BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5.3 sec • Normal: 17 hits • Full charge: 26 hits.

Strong close pressure and supported Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 — EPITAPH COUNTER

Cooldown: about 12 sec • Normal counter window: 5 sec • Full charge ~1.2 sec → about 8 sec.

Epitaph protects against the first real valid attack during the window and attempts to reposition King Crimson behind the attacker when a valid square exists.

It no longer triggers just because an enemy walked nearby; an actual committed attack has to happen. Existing wounds from before activation remain, so this is not a free heal.

Committed player attacks can still trigger the non-damaging counter/reposition even when PvP/Safety separately blocks HP damage.

SKILL 4 TAP — TIME DASH

Tap for a collision-aware dash of about 5.5 tiles. Use it for quick repositioning without committing to full Time Erase.

SKILL 4 FULL — TIME ERASE

Cooldown: about 32 sec • Full charge: about 1.3 sec • Duration: 10 sec.

During Time Erase the real user is protected from normal damage/targeting and can move, while normal Stand skill use is locked. A decoy remains for the outside world.

Press Skill 4 again to exit early.

Time Stop vs Time Erase: if Time Stop is already active/pending, a new full Time Erase cannot start. If Erase was active first, an incoming Time Stop spends its duration against the remaining Erase time: shorter Stop is consumed, longer Stop cancels Erase and continues with leftover time, and an exact match cancels both.
