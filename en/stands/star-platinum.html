STAR PLATINUM

v0.23.0.237

Punch, Barrage and Star Finger use Directional First-Contact. ORA Barrage can activate the forward Barrage Guard.

Skill 1 — ORA PUNCH

Heavy melee • about 2.75 tiles • active 5-stage punch combo.

Skill 2 — ORA BARRAGE

Rapid punches • 15 normal hits / 25 full-charge hits • supports Barrage Clash.

Skill 3 — STAR FINGER

Piercing line attack • about 5 tiles.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / TIME STOP

Tap: collision-aware Time Dash up to about 11.25 tiles • full charge: 5 sec Time Stop.

Aiming in this version

Attacks covered by Directional First-Contact use mouse direction and the skill's real range. The first body intersecting the attack corridor is selected first, while existing obstacles and PvP/Safety rules still apply.
