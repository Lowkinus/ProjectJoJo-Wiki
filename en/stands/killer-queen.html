KILLER QUEEN

ROLE:
Trap / AOE / Autonomous Pressure

Difficulty:
High

Best For:
Players who like traps, area control and using Sheer Heart Attack as a second source of pressure.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.39 sec • Range: about 2.7 tiles • Full charge: about 1.2 sec.

A basic close-range option for when enemies reach you before your bomb plan is ready.

SKILL 2 — BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5.2 sec • Normal: 16 hits • Full charge: 24 hits.

Useful for close pressure and Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 TAP — FIRST BOMB

Tap or partial charge to place First Bomb. Cooldown: about 0.7 sec • Range: about 5.5 tiles.

If you aim at a living target, the bomb attaches and follows that target. Otherwise it is placed at the world position. Press Skill 3 again while a bomb exists to retrieve/remove it.

Bomb attachment itself is utility, so it can still be attached to a PvP/Safety-protected player; protected players take no HP damage when it detonates.

SKILL 4 — DETONATE

Cooldown: about 6.8 sec • Base radius: 2.5 tiles • Full charge: about 1.25 sec, radius about 3.25 tiles plus more damage.

An attached target takes the primary hit while nearby targets take reduced splash. Ground bombs deal the most damage near the center and fall off toward the edge.

SKILL 3 FULL — SHEER HEART ATTACK

Full charge for about 0.9 sec to deploy or remotely recall SHA. This is separate from First Bomb's placement cooldown.

SHA now behaves as its own autonomous unit: it moves, selects targets and attacks without needing constant commands, with smoother movement than older versions.

  • Max HP: 150
  • Search range: ~28 tiles
  • Attack distance: ~2.65 tiles
  • Bomb wind-up: 1.5 sec
  • Attack cooldown: 4 sec
  • Explosion damage: 50% of KQ Detonate
  • Stored repair: +1 HP every 5 sec

There is no passive HP drain while SHA is deployed. HP is lost from incoming damage and from activity: every 2 real explosions cost 1 HP.

The two-explosion counter persists across manual recall/redeploy and resets after SHA is destroyed and fully repaired.

SHA can be damaged by supported Stand/weapon attacks, and The Hand ERASE can destroy it. At 0 HP, the owner's right hand is badly injured and SHA cannot be redeployed until fully repaired.

PvP/Safety: protected players are not selected as SHA targets; if protection changes during wind-up, SHA cancels that player target, and protected players are skipped by splash damage.

Zombie aggro: SHA chases its own targets, but it does not force nearby zombies to switch targets and swarm it. It can guard and pressure an area, but it is not a free horde lure.
