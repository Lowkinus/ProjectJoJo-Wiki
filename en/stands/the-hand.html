THE HAND

ROLE:
Execution / Rescue / Mobility / Position Control

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who like manipulating distance instead of merely dealing damage.

SPACE GAUGE

Maximum:
100

Passive regeneration:
2 points / second

Punch hit:
+5 Gauge

Barrage hit:
+1 Gauge

Costs:

Space Dash:
35

Space Pull:
20

Erase:
35

Notice something?

Pull is cheaper than Dash.

That is intentional.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH

Cooldown:
approximately 0.48 sec

A heavy Punch.

Successful hits also rebuild Space Gauge.

If you emptied the Gauge:

Stop staring at the bar.

Punch something.

SKILL 2 — BARRAGE

Cooldown:
approximately 5.4 sec

Normal:
16 hits

Full:
25 hits

Each successful hit helps refill Space Gauge.

This makes Barrage both:

  • An offensive skill
  • A resource recovery tool

SKILL 3 TAP — SPACE DASH

Tap Skill 3.

Cost:
35 Gauge

The Hand erases space and moves you forward.

The movement is collision-aware.

It does not intentionally send you directly through solid walls.

Use it for:

  • Escaping a horde
  • Closing distance
  • Repositioning
  • Getting through an opening quickly
  • Reaching the friend who ignored every warning you gave them

SKILL 3 FULL — SPACE PULL

Full Charge:
approximately 0.7 sec

Cost:
20 Gauge

Maximum Pull range:
approximately 21.875 tiles

This uses a directional corridor.

You aim toward the target.

You do NOT need to perfectly place the cursor on a single pixel of their body.

Against PvE targets:

Pull can also deal a small amount of damage.

Against players:

Space Pull deals NO HP damage.

Even when it moves them.

This creates several uses people miss:

1. PULL ENEMIES TO YOU

Separate one dangerous target from a group.

Pull it.

Delete it.

Very sophisticated tactical strategy.

Official name:

"Come here."

2. RESCUE TEAMMATES

Friend is surrounded?

Pull them out.

Friend is grabbed?

Pull them away.

Friend is stuck in the wrong side of a fight?

Pull them.

Friend is standing next to Killer Queen's suspicious glowing location?

PULL THEM NOW.

3. CONTROL PVP WITHOUT DAMAGE

PvP/Safety OFF?

You can still reposition your friend/enemy with Pull.

It deals no HP damage to players anyway.

Sometimes the best support Stand is the one whose main ability is kidnapping.

SKILL 4 — ERASE

Cooldown:
approximately 25 sec

Cost:
35 Gauge

Short-range execution.

It can erase supported:

  • Living non-player targets
  • Certain world objects
  • Enemy SHA
  • PvP-valid players

Against a PvP-protected player:

The effect can happen visually, but:

The player is NOT killed.

IMPORTANT:

Erase consumes Gauge even when you deliberately miss.

Aim first.

Then erase.

Do not spend 35 Space Gauge deleting the atmosphere.

Okuyasu already handles that department.
