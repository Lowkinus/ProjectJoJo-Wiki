PASSIVE — SPEED GAUGE

Idle +0.1/sWalk +1/sRun +11/s • Sprint +20/s

Summoning SC gives roughly 1.12x movement speed. Gauge regeneration pauses under Time Stop or THREE FREEZE. This is a Stand where moving is part of the rotation.

Skill 1 — STAB

0.22s cooldown3.1 tiles1.1s full charge

Fast, cheap and costs no Speed Gauge. Use it to finish singles or bridge the time between larger Gauge spends.

Skill 2 — RAPIER RUSH

3s cooldown18 Gauge18 / 26 hits

Full charge takes 1.6s. Main close-range burst, can Barrage Clash, and creates Barrage Guard.

Skill 3 — CIRCLE SWEEP

3.5s cooldown25 Gauge~4.2-tile reach

Your get-off-me button when the crowd starts wrapping around the sides. Full charge takes 1.2s and adds power.

Skill 4 — RAPIER SHOT

5.2s cooldown8.2 tiles1.05s full charge

The rapier flies on the mouse line and can be body-blocked. After firing, the sword is away for about 5.2s and SC skills are locked until it returns. Barrage Guard can deflect the projectile.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

The real rotation

Run to refill → Rush in → Stab to finish or stall → leave and run again. SC gets smoother the more you respect the hit-and-run rhythm.

Save Sweep for the wrap

Spending 25 Gauge on cooldown is wasteful. Hold it until the horde starts folding around your flank and you actually need the breathing room.

Rapier Shot is a commitment

Once the sword leaves, the rest of the kit goes quiet for 5.2 seconds. Fire it when you have distance and a clear escape, not with three zombies touching your shoes.

Guard while entering

Rush gives you Barrage Guard from the front, which helps against supported projectiles. It does nothing for the side or rear — your speed still has to solve those angles.

Keep this in mind: Standing still and waiting for cooldowns is how you turn Silver Chariot into a worse version of itself. Run. That is the reload.
