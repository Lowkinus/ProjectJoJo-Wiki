KILLER QUEEN

v0.23.0.237

Punch, Barrage and First Bomb use Directional First-Contact. Barrage Guard is not enabled for Killer Queen.

PUNCH / BARRAGE

Close pressure and setup.

FIRST BOMB

Attaches the bomb to the real first-contact target.

DETONATE

Detonates the active First Bomb according to KQ rules.

SHEER HEART ATTACK

Autonomous pressure unit that follows current PvP/Safety targeting rules.

Aiming in this version

Attacks covered by Directional First-Contact use mouse direction and the skill's real range. The first body intersecting the attack corridor is selected first, while existing obstacles and PvP/Safety rules still apply.
