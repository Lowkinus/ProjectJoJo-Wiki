CRAZY DIAMOND

ROLE:
Support Bruiser / Healing / Repair

Difficulty:
High if you actually use Restore correctly

Best For:
Co-op groups and players who cannot decide whether they want to be a fighter, doctor or mechanic.

Answer:

Yes.

SKILL 3 — ATTACK / RESTORE MODE

Cooldown:
approximately 0.25 sec

Instantly switches between:

ATTACK MODE

and

RESTORE MODE

This changes the behavior of:

  • Punch
  • Barrage
  • Glass Shot

Punch and Barrage become restoration tools in Restore Mode.

Glass Shot works differently.

Its two modes are connected through a target Mark system.

Learn to switch modes quickly.

Crazy Diamond is not meant to sit in Restore Mode forever.

RESTORE GAUGE

Maximum:
100

Passive regeneration is EXTREMELY slow:

approximately:

+1 point every 100 seconds

You are supposed to recover Restore Gauge by fighting in Attack Mode.

Attack Mode gains:

Punch hit:
+0.5

Barrage hit:
+0.15625 per hit

Glass Shot hit:
+0.625

Restore costs:

Restore Punch:
6.25 on successful heal/repair

Restore Barrage:
1.25 per successful healing tick

If the Restore action does not actually change anything:

The system tries not to waste Gauge on a fake heal.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH / RESTORE PUNCH

Cooldown:
approximately 0.37 sec

Attack Mode:

Strong melee hit.

Restore Mode:

Attempts to heal/repair whatever is under your aim at the actual contact frame.

Normal Restore Punch:
approximately 18 healing power

Full:
approximately 36

You can continue aiming while the punch animation is approaching the contact frame.

This makes Restore feel like an actual Stand interaction instead of selecting a target three business days earlier.

SKILL 2 — DORA BARRAGE / RESTORE BARRAGE

Cooldown:
approximately 5 sec

Attack Mode:

Normal combat Barrage.

Restore Mode:

Repeated healing ticks.

The cursor remains steerable during Restore Rush.

That means you can change which valid target you are restoring during the Barrage.

Useful when multiple teammates are injured.

Doctor Crazy Diamond does house calls.

Violently.

SKILL 4 — GLASS SHOT / GLASS HOMING

ATTACK MODE — GLASS SHOT

Cooldown:
approximately 5.2 sec

Fires Glass normally in the direction of your aim.

Where the Glass ends up becomes your latest:

GLASS MARK

The Mark can be:

  • A living target
  • A player
  • A zombie
  • The ground
  • A wall / door / object
  • The final impact position of the Glass

If Glass Shot hits a living target, the Mark follows that target.

If it does not hit a living target, Crazy Diamond stores the impact position as a fixed World Mark.

Only one Mark can exist at a time.

A new Glass Shot replaces the previous Mark.

GLASS MARK TIMER

A Mark lasts:

60 seconds

If no new Skill 4 use refreshes it, the Mark disappears.

Successfully firing either:

Glass Shot
or
Glass Homing

refreshes the active Mark timer back to:

60 seconds

Glass Shot can also replace it with a new target or impact point.

If a Living Mark dies:

The Mark is immediately lost.

Corpses cannot remain Homing targets.

RESTORE MODE — GLASS HOMING

Cooldown:
approximately 7 sec

Requires an active Glass Mark.

No Mark:

No Glass Homing.

The projectile launches in the direction you are aiming first, then slowly curves toward the Mark.

Glass Homing is intentionally slow.

This gives enemies time to see it coming and lets players react by moving behind cover.

The projectile still physically travels through the world.

If something crosses its path first:

  • Zombie
  • Player
  • Other valid living target
  • Wall
  • Door
  • Window
  • Vehicle
  • Supported obstacle

The Glass hits that first and stops.

It does NOT ignore enemies or walls just because something else is Marked.

A World Mark can therefore be used tactically.

Example:

Mark the ground near a doorway
→ switch to Restore Mode
→ fire Glass Homing
→ a zombie walks across the projectile's path
→ the zombie gets hit instead

You are not only marking enemies.

You can mark where you expect an enemy to be.

Glass Homing uses a distinctive golden effect.

TIP:

A Living Mark is for chasing a target.

A World Mark is for planning ahead.

Sometimes the best target is not where the zombie is.

It is where the zombie is about to be.

CRAZY DIAMOND TEAM TIP

Do NOT waste all your Restore Gauge healing tiny scratches after every fight.

Fight in Attack Mode.

Build Gauge.

Use Glass Shot to mark important targets.

Save Restore for:

  • Serious injuries
  • Team emergencies
  • Vehicle damage
  • Important equipment/world repair
  • A Homing Glass follow-up when you already have a useful target marked

Crazy Diamond becomes much stronger when the player treats Restore Gauge, Mode switching and the Glass Mark as resources instead of free infinite healing.
