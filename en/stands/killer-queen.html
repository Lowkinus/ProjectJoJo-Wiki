PLAYSTYLE • Bomb setup • Detonation • Sheer Heart Attack

KILLER QUEEN

Killer Queen wins by preparing danger before the enemy realizes it. Mark a target/location as a bomb, decide when to detonate, or deploy SHA for autonomous pressure.

Current guide refresh • Core v0.23.0.280
DifficultyMedium
Bomb range5.5 tiles
Detonate radius2.5 base
SHA HP150

Skill 1 — PUNCH

CD 0.39s • Range 2.7

Simple melee for when you need direct pressure instead of setup.

Example: Use it to create space while waiting for the right bomb opportunity.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

CD 5.2s • Range 3.0 • 16 / 24 hits

Sustained close pressure. Useful for holding a target while your bomb plan is already prepared.

Example: Plant first, then use Barrage to keep a tough target from casually walking away before detonation.

Skill 3 — BOMB / HOLD SHA

Tap: First Bomb • Full 0.9s: SHA

Tap/partial uses the First Bomb route. Hold to FULL summons or recalls Sheer Heart Attack. SHA is an autonomous 150-HP unit that moves toward targets, attacks on its own, and can be damaged.

Example: Use SHA to pressure one side while you personally set up a bomb somewhere else.

Skill 4 — DETONATE

CD 3.5s • Range 18 • AOE

Triggers the prepared bomb route and deals heavy area damage. Full charge increases damage/radius.

Example: Wait until more than one threat enters the blast area instead of detonating the instant a single weak zombie touches the bomb.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT THIS STAND

Do not memorize jargon

Focus on the loop: what builds your resource, what spends it, and which skill solves the problem in front of you.

Use the examples

The examples above are not fixed combos. They are situations that explain why the mechanic exists, so you can adapt it to your own fight.
