SPACE GAUGE

Punch +5Barrage +1 / hit+2 / sec passive

Pull costs 20. Dash costs 35. ERASE costs 35. Real offensive hits refill the bar, which is why The Hand wants to fight instead of permanently running away.

Skill 1 — PUNCH

0.48s cooldown2.8 tiles1.25s full charge

A heavy hit and your best single-contact Gauge refill.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

5.4s cooldown3.05 tiles16 / 25 hits

Full charge takes 1.75s. Every real damage tick can feed Space Gauge back into the kit.

Skill 3 TAP — SPACE DASH

2.8s cooldown14-tile command range35 Gauge

Tap to warp along the mouse direction with path checks. If the route is truly blocked and no movement happens, the Dash cost is not burned.

Skill 3 HOLD — SPACE PULL

0.7s full hold21.875 tiles20 Gauge

Pulls the best living target inside the narrow forward corridor. Great for rescue or isolation. A whiff still spends the 20 Gauge.

Skill 4 — ERASE

25s cooldown3.25 tiles35 Gauge

A true close-range contact move. The hand sweep must physically catch the target at the action frame. Missing still costs the resource.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Do the math

Dash 35 + ERASE 35 is 70 Gauge. If you are not sure the play ends there, use Pull or a Punch refill first instead of emptying the tank.

Pull the problem to you

Pull a dangerous target out of the pack → Punch/Barrage to refill → ERASE only when the angle is clean. That is safer than diving into the middle of the horde.

Dash is also the exit

Do not spend your last 35 Gauge just because ERASE is available. Sometimes the smartest Skill 3 is the one that gets you out after a miss.

Rescue tool

Space Pull is utility, not ordinary HP damage. It can be used to drag a teammate out of a bad choke while PvP/Safety handles damage separately.

Keep this in mind: The Hand loses fights by bankrupting itself. Watch the blue bar before the cool animation.
