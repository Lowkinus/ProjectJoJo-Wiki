SILVER CHARIOT

ROLE:
Hit & Run / Mobility / Fast Pressure

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who enjoy movement more than standing in one location holding M1 until something dies.

PASSIVE — MOVEMENT SPEED

While Silver Chariot is summoned:

+12% Movement Speed

Dismiss the Stand and the bonus is removed.

It is designed not to repeatedly multiply itself on top of other speed changes.

SPEED GAUGE

Maximum:
100

Only these skills spend Speed Gauge:

Rapier Barrage — 18
Circle Sweep — 25

Stab does NOT consume Speed Gauge.

Rapier Shot does NOT consume Speed Gauge.

Gauge regeneration depends on your movement:

Standing:
0.1 / sec

Walking:
1 / sec

Running:
11 / sec

Sprinting:
20 / sec

Movement in a vehicle does not count as sprinting around with Silver Chariot.

Nice try.

SKILL 1 — STAB

Cooldown:
approximately 0.22 sec

Range:
approximately 3.1 tiles

This is extremely fast.

Use it for continuous pressure while preserving your Speed Gauge.

CORE IDEA:

Stab is your bread and butter.

Rush/Sweep are the expensive meal.

SKILL 2 — RAPIER BARRAGE

Cooldown:
approximately 3 sec

Gauge Cost:
18

Normal:
18 hits

Full:
26 hits

Fast attack interval and good pressure.

Can Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 — CIRCLE SWEEP

Cooldown:
approximately 3.5 sec

Gauge Cost:
25

360-degree AOE around the user.

This is your:

"I have made a terrible positioning decision"

button.

Use it when zombies begin surrounding you.

Then MOVE.

The entire Stand is trying to teach you this lesson.

SKILL 4 — RAPIER SHOT

Cooldown:
approximately 5.2 sec

Range:
approximately 8.2 tiles

A powerful ranged attack.

After firing:

The sword is unavailable for approximately 5.2 seconds.

During the sword-return lock, Silver Chariot cannot simply continue using its normal skill set.

So do NOT fire the Rapier Shot into one weak zombie while 20 more are entering melee range.

Use it:

  • As a finisher
  • From safe distance
  • Before disengaging
  • When you know you can survive the sword-return window

PLAYSTYLE:

Attack.
Run.
Recover Gauge.
Turn around.
Attack again.

If you are standing still waiting for Gauge:

Polnareff is disappointed in you.
