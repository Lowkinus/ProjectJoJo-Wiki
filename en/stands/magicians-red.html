MAGICIAN'S RED

ROLE:
Ranged Damage / Fire / Horde Control / Utility

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who like controlling groups from range and are prepared to accept that fire has opinions.

FIRE GAUGE

Maximum:
100

Regeneration:
2 / second

0 → 100:
approximately 50 seconds

Skill costs:

Fireball:
8

Flame Spray:
20

Fire Control:
FREE

Cross Fire:
32 normal → 55 full charge

The individual costs are relatively cheap.

The recovery is intentionally slow.

You can burst hard.

You cannot burst forever.

SKILL 1 — FIREBALL

Cooldown:
approximately 1.7 sec

Range:
approximately 8 tiles

Cost:
8 Fire Gauge

Ranged fire attack.

It can also ignite supported utilities.

Examples include supported:

  • Campfires
  • Stoves
  • Fireplaces

Full-charge Fireball can also create ground fire when:

  • It does not hit a living target
  • It did not use the cast to ignite a utility
  • The internal ground-fire cooldown is ready

Ground-fire internal cooldown:
approximately 9 seconds

Therefore Fireball is not only:

"throw fire at zombie."

It is also:

"I forgot the matches."

SKILL 2 — FLAME SPRAY

Cooldown:
approximately 5.2 sec

Range:
approximately 5.2 tiles

Cost:
20

Normal:
22 hits

Full:
32 hits

Excellent sustained fire pressure.

Can ignite supported non-player living targets.

Good against crowds entering a narrow area.

Bad when your tactical plan is:

"My wooden safe house can probably handle this."

SKILL 3 — FIRE CONTROL

Cooldown:
approximately 4.4 sec

Gauge Cost:
0

This skill deals:

NO DAMAGE

Instead it extinguishes:

  • Burning players
  • Friends
  • Zombies / supported living actors
  • Nearby world fire

Full charge increases the effect radius.

This is one of the most useful emergency team skills in the mod.

Friend is burning?

Do not type:

"lol"

Use Skill 3 first.

Then type "lol".

SKILL 4 — CROSS FIRE

Cooldown:
approximately 12 sec

Base radius:
approximately 3.2 tiles

Full charge:
Much larger AOE + increased damage

Full radius reaches roughly:
5.9 tiles

Cross Fire can damage many targets and create fire at the impact location.

This is your major horde-control burst.

Also:

This is your major:

"Why is the neighborhood on fire?"

burst.

Use outside whenever possible.
