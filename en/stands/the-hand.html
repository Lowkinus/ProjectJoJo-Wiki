PLAYSTYLE • Space Gauge • Pull • ERASE

THE HAND

The Hand is about deciding how to spend one shared Space Gauge. Movement, pulling and ERASE are powerful, but they compete for the same resource.

Current guide refresh • Project JoJo Core
DifficultyMedium–High

SPACE GAUGE

Max 100 • +2/sec passive

Punch/Rush can also generate Space. Skill 3 and Skill 4 spend the same pool, so an unnecessary movement can leave you without enough Gauge for ERASE.

Example: Before entering a dangerous room, keep at least 35 Space if you want ERASE available as your emergency answer.

Skill 1 — PUNCH

CD 0.48s • Range 2.8

Normal melee that also helps recover Space through real hits.

Example: Use cheap punches to rebuild Space instead of immediately spending another movement skill.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

CD 5.4s • Range 3.05

Close pressure and another way to earn Space from repeated contact.

Example: Good when you need both damage and resource recovery before the next Pull/Erase.

Skill 3 — SPACE

Tap movement: 35 • Full Pull: 20

Tap spends the larger movement cost; full charge uses the cheaper Pull route to drag a target from long range.

Example: Pull one zombie out of a doorway instead of walking into the whole group.

Skill 4 — ERASE

CD 25s • Range 3.25 • Cost 35

Short-range high-value space erasure. It is strong, but spending the Gauge means you may not have enough left for movement immediately afterward.

Example: Do not ERASE a harmless lone zombie if you are about to enter a larger fight and need the same Gauge for escape.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT THIS STAND

Do not memorize jargon

Focus on the loop: what builds your resource, what spends it, and which skill solves the problem in front of you.

Use the examples

The examples above are not fixed combos. They are situations that explain why the mechanic exists, so you can adapt it to your own fight.
