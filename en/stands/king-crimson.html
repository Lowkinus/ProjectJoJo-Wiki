PLAYSTYLE • Counter attacks • Positioning • Time Erase

KING CRIMSON

Best for players who like timing. Epitaph reacts to a real committed attack, while Time Erase lets you leave normal targeting and rebuild the fight from a better position.

Current guide refresh • Project JoJo Core
DifficultyHigh
Epitaph5 sec window
Time Dash13.75 tiles
Time Erase10 sec

Skill 1 — PUNCH

CD 0.4s • Range 2.9

A strong punish tool after Epitaph or a good reposition.

Example: When Epitaph moves you to a safe angle behind the attacker, punch immediately instead of wasting that free position.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

CD 5.3s • Range 3.15 • 17 hits

Close-range pressure with Barrage Clash/Guard support.

Example: Use it when the counter gives you enough time to stay close; if the angle is bad, do not force it—Dash away.

Skill 3 — EPITAPH COUNTER

CD 12s • 5s base window

Epitaph waits for a real incoming attack, not mere proximity. On a valid threat it tries to reposition you away/behind the attacker.

Example: Use it when you see a zombie/player committing to an attack animation, not randomly while they are still walking.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / TIME ERASE

CD 32s • Tap 13.75 • Hold 10s Erase

Tap repositions. Full charge enters Time Erase: normal Stand skills are locked while you move freely outside normal targeting/damage. Press Skill 4 again to exit early.

Example: Use Erase to cross a bad zone or break a horde’s pressure, then exit at a new angle. Do not spend all 10 seconds standing still.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT THIS STAND

Do not memorize jargon

Focus on the loop: what builds your resource, what spends it, and which skill solves the problem in front of you.

Use the examples

The examples above are not fixed combos. They are situations that explain why the mechanic exists, so you can adapt it to your own fight.
