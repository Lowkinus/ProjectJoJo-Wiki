HOW THE MOD WORKS

You do not need to read five changelogs before throwing your first punch. These are the systems that actually matter in play.

01 • FIND THE ARROWWhere does it drop?

Zombies have a base chance of roughly 1 in 250. Arrows can also appear in world containers. Garages, tool crates and mechanic storage are better hunting grounds; vehicle loot is intentionally rarer.

02 • USE ITThree successful rolls per Arrow

The Arrow uses native Condition: 3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → gone. Condition is spent only after a Stand roll succeeds. Infinite Stand Arrow in Sandbox keeps it from wearing out.

03 • CONTROLSCAPS LOCK + 1 2 3 4

Caps Lock summons or dismisses your Stand. Use the top-row 1–4 keys for skills. Tap and full-hold can be completely different moves, so charging everything is not always the smart play.

04 • LEVEL UPLv.1 is only half power

Each Stand keeps its own Level. Lv.1 starts at about 50% of balanced damage and scales to 100% at Lv.100. Stand-caused zombie kills give EXP. Rerolling does not erase the saved level of your old Stand.

05 • AIMINGThe mouse gives direction, not a magic lock

v237 uses Directional First-Contact. Skills travel along your mouse line at their real range. The first body that actually touches that corridor gets the hit.

06 • BARRAGESteer it. Do not just hold it.

Barrages can turn with the mouse. The contact system keeps a short ~240 ms empty-frame grace and can catch up by up to 3 hits on a slow update. Offensive Barrages can also Clash.

07 • DEFLECTBarrage Guard

SP / TW / attack-mode CD / SC / KC can put a forward guard line in front of their Barrage. It is about 2.5 tiles wide total, and only stops projectiles that truly cross it from the front.

08 • PVPSafety protects HP, not every trick

Player damage still obeys Project Zomboid PvP/Safety. Non-damaging Stand mechanics can have their own rules, so things like THREE FREEZE, Space Pull and Epitaph are not the same thing as a normal damage hit.

Want the machinery? Time Stop, Time Erase, Barrage Clash, Guard, Stand Level and Sandbox rules are in the full system guide. Combos and real play patterns live at the bottom of every Stand page.

Know the rules before the pose

CORE COMBAT SYSTEMS

FIRST-CONTACT

A close cursor no longer cuts down a long skill. The move uses its real maximum reach and checks the first body on the attack line. Walls, doors, windows and vehicles still matter.

BARRAGE CLASH

Supported offensive Barrages can collide when players are within about 5.25 tiles and are genuinely facing/aiming into each other. Clash hits cancel against each other before normal damage.

BARRAGE GUARD

Direct support includes TW Knife, SC Rapier Shot and CD Glass/Homing. Vanilla firearms have a native per-hit hook, but live B42 validation across SP / Host / Dedicated is still required before calling firearm coverage complete.

TIME STOP

SP gets 5 seconds. TW gets 9. Damage dealt to frozen targets is banked and released when time moves again. SP/TW users caught inside another stop can fully charge their own Time Stop to earn a finite movement window.

STOP vs ERASE

An active or pending Time Stop blocks a new full Time Erase. If Erase existed first, the incoming Stop spends its duration against the remaining Erase time. Only the excess becomes actual stopped time.

SANDBOX

Server owners can tune sound volume, Arrow drops, Stand damage, Stand Level, test Arrows and Infinite Stand Arrow. Make it generous. Make it cruel. That part is yours.
