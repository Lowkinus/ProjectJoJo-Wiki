HARVEST

Shigechi's colony Stand fights very differently from the heavy hitters. You manage a swarm of up to 500 Harvest, spend units to harass enemies, commit groups to delayed attacks, send crews away to search for marked items, and flood the area with a long-running Storm. The swarm is powerful because it is everywhere — but every unit you send away is one less unit you can use right now.

THE HARVEST SWARM — 0 / 500

Maximum 500Movement = no regeneration0.65s stillness graceSearch lowers the regeneration cap

HARVEST uses a real colony count instead of a normal energy bar. Skills 1–4 all draw from the same swarm. Units committed to Search are temporarily away from you and cannot be regenerated over until they return.

Example: send 100 Harvest on Search and your current regeneration ceiling becomes 400 / 500. The HUD shows a red −100 beside the gauge so you always know how many units are working away from you.

SWARM REGENERATION

Still: 0.80 / secSeated / Vehicle: 4.05 / secRest: 9.00 / secSleep: 14.40 / sec

Walking or running stops swarm regeneration. After standing still for 0.65 seconds, Harvest begins returning slowly. Sitting is much faster; Rest and Sleep are the strongest recovery states.

The HARVEST Sandbox option Harvest Regeneration Speed (%) ranges from 25% to 500%. The default is 100%, which uses the values above. This changes regeneration speed only — it does not shorten Search time and it never restores Harvest that are still reserved on a Search job.

Skill 1 — HARVEST SHOT

Cost 1 Harvest0.26s CDRange 90.045 raw damage

Fire one Harvest toward the cursor. Tap for a single shot or hold the skill to keep firing at the cooldown rate. It is deliberately light on raw damage, but repeated impacts provide fast ranged harassment and small knockback / mini-CC that can keep the front of a horde from walking cleanly into you.

Barrage Guard: this projectile is guardable through Project JoJo's shared Barrage Guard rules.

Skill 2 — NEEDLE SWARM

Minimum 7 Harvest1.0s CDRange 69.0s latch

Release the skill to send the selected group onto one target. The swarm clings to that target for 9 seconds, then stabs together. Holding the skill adds Harvest one unit at a time; the +1 steps accelerate the longer you hold.

At the minimum 7-Harvest spend, the strike deals roughly 0.145 raw damage. Committing more Harvest increases the damage continuously, reaching up to 0.650 raw at 500 Harvest.

If the target dies, disappears or breaks the leash before the stab resolves, the pending swarm is cancelled and those committed units are refunded. A successful stab consumes them.

Skill 3 — SEARCH ITEM

Minimum 20 Harvest0.8s CD20 = 1.0× Search500 = 7.2× Search

Right-click an item in your inventory and choose HARVEST's Mark action. HARVEST remembers one marked item at a time; marking a new item immediately replaces the old mark. Skill 3 then sends a selected search crew to look for another copy of that marked item.

Search crews are reserved until they physically return and finish delivery. They lower your active regeneration ceiling while away. Multiple Search jobs stack their reservations.

Skill 4 — HARVEST STORM

Minimum 33 Harvest1.2s CD3.4 area0.5s damage ticks

Commit a group of Harvest to swarm around you as sustained area pressure. A minimum 33-Harvest Storm lasts about 14 seconds. Committing more Harvest increases its duration smoothly until a full 500-Harvest Storm reaches the 38-second cap.

Storm is not a burst nuke. Its strength is repeated area hits, small knockback / mini-CC and the ability to disrupt several enemies at once. It can affect up to 16 targets per area hit. You may still use Skills 1–3 from the Harvest left in your active pool, but normal swarm regeneration pauses while Storm is active.

Press Skill 4 again to end Storm early. Barrage Guard can block Storm hits on the guarding player without cancelling the Storm for every other target in the area.

HOLD ALLOCATION

Tap

Uses the skill's minimum amount: Needle 7, Search 20, Storm 33.

Hold

Adds exactly +1 Harvest per step. The steps become faster over time; the game never jumps +5 or +10 in one tick.

Release

The selected amount is committed only when the skill is actually released / used.

SEARCH LIFECYCLE

Dismiss HARVEST

Existing Search jobs keep working because those units are already away on their task.

Change / Remove Stand

Search jobs are cancelled and reserved Harvest are refunded so another Stand cannot be used while HARVEST keeps farming.

Death

Search jobs are cancelled without delivering the item; reserved Harvest are returned before the jobs are cleared.

Reconnect

Search progress is preserved, but offline time does not advance the timer. Returning / delivery phases resume safely instead of duplicating rewards.

HOW TO PLAY HARVEST

Keep a reserve

Do not send all 500 units away unless the area is truly safe. A 500-unit Search is fast, but you are giving up every HARVEST combat skill until the crew comes back.

S1 buys distance

Use rapid HARVEST SHOT impacts to interrupt the front line while you reposition. Its low damage is intentional; the spacing is the real value.

S2 is commitment

Needle is delayed single-target pressure. Larger allocations matter when you have nine seconds to let the swarm finish the job.

S4 controls a horde

Storm is strongest when several zombies are trying to enter the same space. Repeated mini-CC gives you room to escape, reload, heal or change the fight.

Remember: HARVEST is strongest when you manage the colony instead of emptying it. Search, Needle and Storm all compete for the same 500-unit swarm.
