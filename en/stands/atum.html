ATUM

ATUM wins through prediction rather than raw damage. Read an attack, mark non-player souls, then force a real contacted target into RPS, Baseball or Racing.

Current addon guide • v0.24.0.069
HOW TO GET ATUM

Use the normal Project JoJo Stand Arrow. ATUM joins the registered Stand pool when this addon is enabled. Super Arrow can list it directly if the server has that option enabled.

CONTROLS

Caps Lock — Summon/Dismiss
1 — Punch
2 — Barrage
3 — Mind Read
4 — Gamble

SKILL 3 — MIND READ

Tap/release before FULL spends 1 Mind Point to arm a 10-second defensive read. A successful read against a non-player attacker can apply a Soul Mark for 3 minutes. Hold until FULL to prepare the YES/NO prediction route for Skill 4. Cancelling an unused read before timeout refunds the point.

SKILL 4 — GAMBLE

Tap Skill 4 and make real contact to start one random game: Rock Paper Scissors, Baseball or Racing. Hold Skill 4 to FULL to toggle AUTO GAMBLE PASSIVE. FULL S3 → Tap 4 adds the prediction route.

SOUL MARK

A successful defensive Mind Read can mark a non-player soul. When a Gamble starts, eligible marked non-player souls near the main target can be bound into the same wager. Players are not Soul Marked.

MIND GAUGE

Mind Gauge is 0–100. Skill 3 costs 1. RPS/Baseball win gives +0 to +3, Racing win +2 to +5, and a correct FULL-S3 prediction plus win gives +1 extra.

WINNER'S HIGH — NOW A FULL MOOD CLEAN

The actual human winner has Stress, Panic, Unhappiness and Boredom cleared to 0. If the current game/framework exposes Anger or Fear through the supported stats path, those are also cleared. This does not heal injuries, Hunger, Thirst, Fatigue or Pain.

MINIGAME PROTECTION

Participants are temporarily protected/phased from normal outside combat while the minigame is active. Visibility/protection is restored when the session ends.
