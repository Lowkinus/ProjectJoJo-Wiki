THE WORLD

v0.23.0.237

Punch, Barrage and Knife use First-Contact. MUDA Barrage can activate Barrage Guard.

Skill 1 — MUDA PUNCH

Heavy single punch • about 2.8 tiles.

Skill 2 — MUDA BARRAGE

15 normal hits / 28 full-charge hits • supports Barrage Clash.

Skill 3 — KNIFE THROW

Projectile follows the actual mouse direction • about 9 tiles.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / ZA WARUDO

Tap: Time Dash up to about 13.125 tiles • full charge: 9 sec Time Stop.

Aiming in this version

Attacks covered by Directional First-Contact use mouse direction and the skill's real range. The first body intersecting the attack corridor is selected first, while existing obstacles and PvP/Safety rules still apply.
