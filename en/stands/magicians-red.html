FIRE GAUGE

Fireball is freeFlame Spray costs 20Cross costs 32 → 55

The bar regenerates at +2 per second. A real damaging Fireball hit adds another +4, making accurate S1 use the engine that pays for the expensive fire.

Skill 1 — FIREBALL

1.7s cooldown8 tiles1.05s full charge

A straight physical projectile: first body in the line can take it. A full charge can create ground fire when it does not hit an actor/utility and the separate 9-second ground-fire cooldown is ready. It can also ignite supported utility targets.

Skill 2 — FLAME SPRAY

5.2s cooldown5.2 tiles22 / 32 hits • 20 Gauge

A long-range fire Barrage for lanes and choke points. It can ignite supported targets. Magician’s Red does not get Barrage Guard.

Skill 3 — FIRE CONTROL

4.4s cooldown10-tile cast range3.2 / 4.32 radius

Free, non-damaging utility. Extinguishes world fire and burning actors, including players. Full charge takes 1.15s and increases radius.

Skill 4 — CROSS FIRE BOMB

12s cooldown8 tiles3.2 → 5.92 radius

Your main area burst. Gauge cost scales from 32 to 55 with charge. Full charge takes 1.45s and expands both impact and footprint. This area-fire move is intentionally not bound by the same straight projectile wall rule.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Earn the fire first

Land Fireballs for +4 Gauge, then spend on Spray/Cross. If every fight starts with S4, your bar will disappear before the crowd does.

Choke points love you

Doors, fences and narrow lanes make Fireball + Flame Spray much stronger because the horde solves your aiming problem for you.

Charge only what you need

Cross Fire scales its cost with charge. If the base radius already covers the pack, a max charge is just paying extra for empty floor.

Use Fire Control

When your base, your friend or your escape path catches fire, press Skill 3 first. The dramatic explanation can happen after the flames are gone.

Keep this in mind: Fire controls space, including space you planned to walk through later. Wooden bases and narrow exits do not care whose Stand started it.
