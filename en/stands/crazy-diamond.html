RESTORE GAUGE

Punch +0.30Rush +0.09375 / hitGlass +0.375

Passive regen is intentionally tiny — about one point every 100 seconds. Offensive contact is how you refill. Restore Punch costs 6.25, Restore Rush costs 1.25 per successful heal tick, and Glass Homing costs 6.25.

Skill 1 — DORAAA / RESTORE PUNCH

0.37s cooldown2.8 tiles1.2s full charge

Attack Mode is your normal punch. Restore Mode heals about 18, or roughly 36 fully charged, and only spends Gauge when the Restore succeeds. Crazy Diamond does not use this move to heal itself.

Skill 2 — DORA RUSH / RESTORE RUSH

5s cooldown17 / 25 hitsAttack = Guard

Attack Mode is an offensive Barrage and creates Barrage Guard. Restore Mode heals about 4 per successful tick, roughly 8.4 at full charge, spending 1.25 Gauge per real heal tick. Restore Rush does not Barrage Clash.

Skill 3 — ATTACK / RESTORE MODE

0.25s cooldownInstant toggleChanges Skills 1/2/4

Attack Mode is how you generate pressure and Gauge. Restore Mode turns Punch/Rush into recovery tools and transforms Glass Shot into Glass Homing.

Skill 4 ATTACK — GLASS SHOT

5.2s cooldown9 tiles60s Mark

A real hit marks that actor. A miss that reaches world geometry marks the impact point instead. Only one Mark exists at a time.

Skill 4 RESTORE — GLASS HOMING

7s cooldown6.25 Gauge7.15 tiles/sec

Requires a valid Mark on roughly the same floor. The shard launches along your raw mouse direction first, then curves back toward the Mark. It travels physically, so walls, vehicles and other bodies can intercept it. A successful cast refreshes the Mark to 60 seconds.

Restore is bigger than healing: current support code can also restore several world targets such as vehicle parts, item condition, food state, doors, windows, barricades and other supported objects.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

The real loop

Attack Mode → land hits → build Gauge → switch to Restore when there is a reason → return to Attack before you drain dry. That loop is the Stand.

Glass setup

Land Glass Shot on the target you care about → switch Restore → fire Homing from an angle. The shard still has to travel, so body-blocking and walls remain real counterplay.

Do not overheal the bar

Dumping the whole Gauge into Restore Rush feels heroic until nobody has Gauge left for the next emergency. Keep enough for at least one useful Punch or Homing cast.

Guard has a mode

If you need Barrage Guard, be in Attack Mode. Restore Rush does not create it. Switching at the wrong moment can turn a knife into a very educational projectile.

Keep this in mind: Passive Restore regen is basically pocket change. If you try to play pure healer without fighting, the Gauge will eventually tell you no.
