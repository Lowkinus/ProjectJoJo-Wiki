ECHOES ACT 3

v0.23.0.237

Barrage/THREE FREEZE use First-Contact. Barrage Guard is not enabled for Echoes ACT 3.

BARRAGE

Base damage per hit increased 0.026 → 0.060 (~2.31×) before Level/Crit/Sandbox multipliers.

HIT COUNT

14 normal hits • charge can increase it to 23 hits.

THREE FREEZE

Single-target control and team setup.

CONTROL ROLE

Still CC-focused, but Barrage is no longer as weak as before.

Aiming in this version

Attacks covered by Directional First-Contact use mouse direction and the skill's real range. The first body intersecting the attack corridor is selected first, while existing obstacles and PvP/Safety rules still apply.
