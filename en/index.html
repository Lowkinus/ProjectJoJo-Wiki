Configure before combat

CLIENT OPTIONS & SANDBOX

Stand Targeting Mode

Mouse / Pointer uses cursor direction • Facing Direction uses where the character faces • Controller Aim uses the controller aim/attack axis and falls back to facing direction inside the dead-zone.

Example: If you use Project Viewpoint and do not want to depend on a visible cursor, Facing Direction lets attacks follow the character. Walls and bodies in front still matter.

HUD / Personal audio

Choose Normal / Minimal / Hidden, HUD opacity 20–100%, scale 70–150%, and personal Project JoJo volume 0–100%. These are per-player preferences, not server balance settings.

Required gameplay screens such as HARVEST Search, ATUM minigames and Heaven's Door Book/Command remain available even with the passive HUD hidden.

Super Arrow

OFF by default. With it off, a Stand Arrow randomly rolls from the loaded Stand Pool. When enabled, using an Arrow opens a choice list made from Stands that are actually registered, including enabled official addons.

Important: Super Arrow changes random selection into direct selection. It does not make the Arrow infinite. The normal 3 successful uses still apply unless Infinite Stand Arrow is also enabled.

Arrow Drop Multiplier

Controls how common Stand Arrows are. At the default 1x setting, the base zombie drop is roughly 1 in 250, and Arrows can also appear in world loot.

Stand Damage

Changes overall Stand damage for the world/server. Use this when you want Stands stronger or weaker without editing each move separately.

Stand Level System

ON = each Stand keeps separate Lv.1–100 progression. OFF = Stands operate at full power while saved progression remains preserved.

Example: Star Platinum Lv.70 and The World Lv.20 are separate. Switching does not erase Star Platinum's Lv.70.

Give Stand Arrow on Join

Optionally gives players an Arrow when joining, useful for servers that do not want the whole group farming before they can use the mod.

Infinite Stand Arrow

Normally one Arrow has three successful uses: 3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → gone. Infinite Arrow prevents Condition loss after a successful use.

Reset Options / Keybinds

Reset Visual / Audio / Targeting restores HUD mode, opacity, scale, personal volume and targeting without touching keybinds. Reset Keybinds separately restores Caps Lock and Skills 1–4.

Shared combat rules

WHAT TO KNOW BEFORE THE HORDE

DIRECTIONAL FIRST-CONTACT

Skills use their real reach and check the first valid contact on the attack line. Pointing farther away does not let a move magically skip the body or wall in front.

BARRAGE CLASH

Supported offensive Barrages can collide when fighters are close enough and genuinely facing/aiming into each other. Colliding hits cancel before normal damage.

BARRAGE GUARD

Supported Barrages create a forward defense line that can stop specific projectiles such as TW Knife, SC Rapier Shot and CD Glass. Side/rear shots and area effects follow different rules.

TIME STOP

SP stops time for 5 seconds and TW for 9. Frozen actors, delayed damage and moving projectiles resume when time starts again.

TIME STOP vs TIME ERASE

The two time systems do not stack freely. If Erase started first, incoming Stop duration is spent against remaining Erase time before any leftover becomes real stopped time.

SUMMONED STAND DEFENSE

While a registered Stand is summoned, its owner takes 33% less net damage. This is damage reduction, not invulnerability.

PvP / SAFETY

Player HP damage respects Project Zomboid PvP/Safety. Some utility/CC effects have separate rules, so check a Stand page before testing pulls, freezes or repositioning on friends.

VEHICLE / WORLD SAFETY

Attacks still respect vehicles and world collision, while safeguards prevent rapid Stand hits from corrupting unsupported vehicle/custom-object state.
