THE ARROW PICKED YOU. NOW WHAT?

BEGINNER GUIDE

No patch-note archaeology. No source-code lecture. Learn the things that matter, then go read the page for the Stand you actually rolled.

BUILD 42+SP / HOST / MPCORE v0.23.0.250
01 • STAND ARROW

FIND THE ARROW

Zombies have a base drop chance of roughly 1 in 250. Arrows can also appear in world containers. Garages, tool crates and mechanic storage are better places to hunt; vehicle loot is intentionally rarer.

02 • USE IT

RANDOM BY DEFAULT — OR CHOOSE WITH SUPER ARROW

The Arrow uses native Condition: 3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → gone. Condition is spent only after a Stand assignment succeeds. By default the Arrow rolls a random registered Stand.

Server/world owners can enable Super Arrow in Sandbox. When enabled, using a Stand Arrow opens a choice list instead of rolling randomly. The list is built from the Stands actually registered in the current game, so enabled official addons such as ATUM, HARVEST and HEAVEN'S DOOR appear automatically. It does not grant addon Stands that are not installed/enabled.

Super Arrow does not make the Arrow infinite. Condition still drops normally unless Infinite Stand Arrow is also enabled.

03 • CONTROLS

SUMMON. THEN START PRESSING THINGS.

Caps Lock summons or dismisses your Stand. Top-row 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 are your skills.

Tap and full-hold can be entirely different moves. Time Dash can become Time Stop. The Hand's Dash can become Pull. Killer Queen's First Bomb can become SHA. Holding everything forever is not a strategy.

All Project JoJo client controls and personal display/aim settings are under Options → Mods → Project JoJo.

04 • STAND LEVEL

LV.1 IS NOT YOUR FINAL FORM

Every Stand keeps its own Lv.1–100 progression. Lv.1 starts around 50% balanced damage and climbs to full power at Lv.100. Stand-caused zombie kills award EXP, and rerolling does not erase the progress of a Stand you used before.

While a registered Stand is summoned, its owner also receives 33% less net damage.

05 • TARGETING MODE

YOU DO NOT HAVE TO AIM WITH THE MOUSE

Open Options → Mods → Project JoJo → Stand Targeting Mode. There are three choices:

  • Mouse / Pointer — default behavior. Attacks use the cursor direction.
  • Facing Direction — ignores the cursor for combat direction and attacks straight where your character is facing. This is useful for keyboard/controller play, or for immersive camera/view mods such as Project Viewpoint where you may intentionally hide or stop relying on the mouse cursor.
  • Controller Aim — follows Project Zomboid's controller aiming axis / attack indicator. If the stick sits inside the dead-zone, it falls back to Facing Direction.

Changing the input source does not turn attacks into magic auto-lock. Project JoJo still uses Directional First-Contact: the move keeps its real range, the first valid body/contact on the attack line wins, and walls, doors, windows and vehicles can still block attacks that should be blocked.

06 • BARRAGE

THE RUSH FOLLOWS YOUR SELECTED AIM MODE

In Mouse mode, Barrage follows the cursor while active. In Facing Direction, it follows the direction your character is facing. In Controller Aim, it follows the controller aim vector. The system keeps about 240 ms of old contact through a tiny empty gap and may catch up by up to 3 hits per update on a slow frame.

When supported offensive Barrages meet at the right distance and angle, they can enter a Barrage Clash instead of politely phasing through each other.

07 • BARRAGE GUARD

YES, YOU CAN PUNCH SOME PROJECTILES AWAY

SP / TW / attack-mode Crazy Diamond / Silver Chariot / King Crimson create a forward guard line during Barrage, roughly 2.5 tiles wide total.

The projectile has to genuinely cross that line from the front. Side and rear shots still hurt, and explosions, area fire or THREE FREEZE are not going to respect your dramatic punching.

08 • PERSONAL HUD / AUDIO

MAKE THE HUD FIT YOUR SCREEN

The same Project JoJo options page lets each player choose Normal / Minimal / Hidden Stand HUD, HUD opacity, HUD scale and personal Project JoJo volume. These are client preferences and do not change the server's balance settings.

Minimal keeps the Stand name and Skill 1–4 cooldown information. Hidden removes the passive Stand HUD, but required gameplay interfaces such as ATUM minigames and HARVEST Search Command remain available.

09 • RESET SETTINGS

CHANGED TOO MUCH? RESET ONLY WHAT YOU WANT.

Reset Visual / Audio / Targeting restores HUD Mode, HUD opacity/scale, personal volume and Targeting Mode without touching your keybinds.

Reset Keybinds separately restores Caps Lock and top-row 1 / 2 / 3 / 4.

10 • PVP / SAFETY

SAFETY PROTECTS HP. STANDS HAVE OTHER IDEAS.

Stand HP damage respects Project Zomboid PvP/Safety. Non-damaging utility and CC can have separate rules, including things such as THREE FREEZE, Space Pull and Epitaph.

If you're playing with friends, read your Stand page before discovering the rule by launching somebody across the room.

That's enough to start.
