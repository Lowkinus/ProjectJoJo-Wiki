COMMON RULES IN PLAIN WORDS

THE THINGS EVERY PLAYER SHOULD KNOW

DIRECTIONAL FIRST-CONTACT

Your attack follows your selected aim direction and hits the first valid body on that line. Example: if two zombies stand in a row, the front one blocks a normal punch from magically selecting the one behind it.

BARRAGE GUARD

Some Barrages create a guard in front of you. A supported projectile must cross that front guard to be stopped; shots from the side or rear still hit.

TIME STOP

Frozen targets cannot move. Damage dealt during stopped time is saved and applied when time starts again. Projectiles inside the stopped area also pause and continue afterward.

STAND LEVEL

Each Stand levels separately from 1 to 100. Switching Stand does not erase the level you already earned on another Stand.
