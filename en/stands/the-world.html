PLAYSTYLE • Heavy melee • Knives • 9-second Time Stop

THE WORLD

A straightforward powerhouse: stronger close-range hits, a 9-tile knife for distance, and the longest Core Time Stop.

Current guide refresh • Core v0.23.0.280
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Knife range9 tiles
Time Stop9 sec
Best atBurst + control

Skill 1 — MUDA PUNCH

CD 0.39s • Range 2.8 • Full 1.2s

Heavy close-range punch. Full charge trades speed for a stronger hit.

Example: Use it after Time Dash to instantly punish a target you reached from far away.

Skill 2 — MUDA BARRAGE

CD 5.2s • Range 3.1 • 15 / 28 hits

Reliable close pressure with Barrage Clash/Guard support. Full charge increases both hit count and damage.

Example: Use Barrage when a single punch is not enough to hold a tough enemy in front of you.

Skill 3 — KNIFE THROW

CD 1.6s • Range 9 • Full 0.9s

A real projectile that can be intercepted by supported Barrage Guard. During your active Time Stop, a FULL-charged throw creates three parallel knives in a front row for the visual attack while gameplay damage remains one attack.

Example: Stop time, line up the target, then full-charge the knife to get the three-knife front-row presentation without accidentally tripling balance damage.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / ZA WARUDO

CD 42s • Tap Dash 13.125 • Hold 9s Stop

Tap is a huge reposition; full charge gives 9 seconds of stopped time. Moving projectiles caught in the stop pause and continue after time resumes.

Example: Save the 42-second ultimate for situations where nine seconds changes the whole fight: rescue, escape, or clearing a blocked route.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT THIS STAND

Do not memorize jargon

Focus on the loop: what builds your resource, what spends it, and which skill solves the problem in front of you.

Use the examples

The examples above are not fixed combos. They are situations that explain why the mechanic exists, so you can adapt it to your own fight.
