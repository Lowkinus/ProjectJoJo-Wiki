Skill 1 — PUNCH

0.39s cooldown2.7 tiles1.2s full charge

Your emergency close-range answer. Useful, but the Stand is built around bombs, not winning a boxing tournament.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

5.2s cooldown3.0 tiles16 / 24 hits

Full charge takes 1.7s. It can Barrage Clash, but Killer Queen does not get Barrage Guard.

Skill 3 TAP — FIRST BOMB

0.7s placement cooldown5.5 tilesPress again to retrieve

If the first valid body on the mouse corridor is a valid offensive target, the Bomb attaches. Otherwise it sits at the world point. PvP/Safety-protected players are ignored by offensive aim selection instead of stealing your mark.

Skill 3 HOLD — SHEER HEART ATTACK

0.9s full hold~28-tile search150 HP

Full hold summons/recalls SHA independently from the First Bomb placement cooldown. SHA moves and targets on its own, attacks around 2.65 tiles, winds up for 1.5s and has a 4s attack cooldown.

SHA — DURABILITY

Stored: +1 HP / 5sEvery 2 explosions: -1 HPExplosion = 50% Detonate

No idle drain. SHA still has real durability and can be damaged by supported Stand/weapon hits. If it reaches 0 HP, the owner takes a serious right-hand penalty and has to wait for repairs.

Skill 4 — DETONATE

6.8s cooldown2.5 / 3.25 radius1.25s full charge

An attached victim takes the primary hit while nearby targets take weaker splash. A ground Bomb uses distance falloff from the center. Full charge boosts damage and radius.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Think in lanes

First-Contact means the body in front wins. Instead of fighting that rule, plant the Bomb on the front body or the choke point and let the crowd carry itself into your setup.

Bomb, leave, explode

You do not need to stand beside your Bomb. Attach/place it, create distance, wait for bodies to bunch up, then Detonate when the blast is worth the cooldown.

Two threats, one owner

Let SHA pressure one side while you set First Bomb on another. Just remember SHA has health — recalling it to repair is part of the kit, not cowardice.

Barrage is the backup plan

If the map is too tight for safe explosions, Barrage is your normal pressure tool. Do not force a Bomb just because the button exists.

Keep this in mind: Killer Queen needs setup time. If you play it like a pure punch Stand, you are volunteering to ignore its best mechanic.
