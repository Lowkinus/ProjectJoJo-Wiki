Skill 1 — PUNCH

0.4s cooldown2.9 tiles1.2s full charge

A heavy punish button. It is at its best after Epitaph hands you a better position.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

5.3s cooldown3.15 tiles17 / 26 hits

Full charge takes 1.8s. It can Barrage Clash and creates Barrage Guard while active.

Skill 3 — EPITAPH

12s cooldown5s base window8s full-charge window

Epitaph does not fire because someone walked near you. The attacker must commit a real attack. It catches the first real threat in the window and tries to reposition you behind or away from the attacker. It does not heal old wounds.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / TIME ERASE

32s cooldownTap: 13.75-tile DashHold: 10s Erase

Full charge takes 1.3s. During Time Erase the real KC owner is removed from normal targeting/damage while moving freely, but normal Stand skills are locked. Press Skill 4 again to leave early.

Time war rule: an active/pending Time Stop blocks a new Erase. If Erase was already running, the Stop spends its own duration against the remaining Erase time. Shorter loses, equal cancels, longer leaves only the excess as stopped time.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Epitaph is a read, not armor

Press it when you expect a committed hit, not when something merely walks close. A correct read gives you a free angle. A random press gives you a 12-second lesson.

Counter into punish

Epitaph triggers → check where you landed → Punch or Barrage immediately if the angle is good. If the warp is awkward, Dash and reset instead of forcing it.

Erase can be defensive

Use Time Erase to cut danger, cross a bad zone, break pressure or flip sides around a horde. You can exit early; there is no prize for standing around until all ten seconds expire.

Change the situation

You cannot use normal Stand skills during Erase. The point is not to wait — the point is to make the battlefield look different when you return.

Keep this in mind: KC looks weak when Epitaph is mashed and Erase is used as a ten-second idle animation. This Stand lives on timing.
