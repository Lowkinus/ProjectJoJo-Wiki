PLAYSTYLE • Close-range fighter • Rising Critical chance • Time Stop

STAR PLATINUM

Fight up close, build Critical chance by landing non-critical ORA hits, then use that critical to hit much harder and launch the target away.

Current guide refresh • Core v0.23.0.280
DifficultyMedium
Crit chance15% → 50%
Critical damage×3.5
Time Stop5 sec

Skill 1 — ORA PUNCH

CD 0.36s • Range 2.75 • Full 1.2s

Normal punch can chain through a five-step combo. Non-critical real Punch hits raise SP Critical chance by +1 percentage point. A critical resets the chance to 15%.

Example: If your Crit chance is already high, save the hit for a dangerous zombie instead of wasting the critical on a target that is almost dead.

Critical launch

8-tile launch • Wall/actor impact

A critical ORA Punch launches the victim up to about 8 tiles and makes it fall at the end. Hitting a wall deals extra impact damage. Hitting another living target hurts both and can cause a small nearby AOE reaction.

Example: Punch a zombie in a doorway: a critical can throw it backward into the group behind it instead of only damaging the first body.

Skill 2 — ORA BARRAGE

CD 5s • Range 3.1 • 15 / 25 hits

Rush builds Critical chance quickly. If any Rush hit rolls a Critical, the barrage does not throw the victim away immediately; the launch is saved for the final Rush hit so the combo can finish first.

Example: Use Barrage on a tough target to build Crit without ruining the Rush by launching it away on the first lucky hit.

Skill 3 — STAR FINGER

CD 7s • Range 5 • Full 1.05s

A straight piercing attack. It can use the current SP Critical chance. A Star Finger critical uses the same launch system as a critical ORA Punch.

Example: Use it when the target is too dangerous to stand next to, especially after you have built a high Crit chance.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / TIME STOP

CD 35s • Tap Dash 11.25 • Hold 5s Stop

Tap is a long reposition. Full charge starts a 5-second Time Stop. Damage on frozen targets is banked, and SP Critical launch waits until time resumes before the body flies.

Example: Decide the plan before stopping time: move into position, place damage, then leave yourself a safe exit instead of spending half the five seconds thinking.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT THIS STAND

Do not memorize jargon

Focus on the loop: what builds your resource, what spends it, and which skill solves the problem in front of you.

Use the examples

The examples above are not fixed combos. They are situations that explain why the mechanic exists, so you can adapt it to your own fight.
