THE ARROW PICKED YOU. NOW WHAT?

BEGINNER GUIDE

No patch-note archaeology. No source-code lecture. Learn the eight things that matter, then go read the page for the Stand you actually rolled.

v0.23.0.237BUILD 42+SP / HOST / MP
01 • STAND ARROW

FIND THE ARROW

Zombies have a base drop chance of roughly 1 in 250. Arrows can also appear in world containers. Garages, tool crates and mechanic storage are better places to hunt; vehicle loot is intentionally rarer.

02 • USE IT

THREE SUCCESSFUL ROLLS

The Arrow uses native Condition: 3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → gone. Condition is spent only after a Stand roll succeeds. Infinite Stand Arrow in Sandbox stops the wear.

03 • CONTROLS

SUMMON. THEN START PRESSING THINGS.

Caps Lock summons or dismisses your Stand. Top-row 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 are your skills.

Tap and full-hold can be entirely different moves. Time Dash can become Time Stop. The Hand's Dash can become Pull. Killer Queen's First Bomb can become SHA. Holding everything forever is not a strategy.

04 • STAND LEVEL

LV.1 IS NOT YOUR FINAL FORM

Every Stand keeps its own Lv.1–100 progression. Lv.1 starts around 50% balanced damage and climbs to full power at Lv.100. Stand-caused zombie kills award EXP, and rerolling does not erase the progress of a Stand you used before.

05 • AIMING

THE MOUSE GIVES DIRECTION, NOT DESTINY

v237 uses Directional First-Contact. The move keeps its real range and travels along your mouse direction. The first body crossing that attack corridor takes the contact. If a zombie is standing in front of your dream target, congratulations: that zombie volunteered.

06 • BARRAGE

STEER THE RUSH

Barrages follow the mouse while active. The system keeps about 240 ms of old contact through a tiny empty gap and may catch up by up to 3 hits per update on a slow frame.

When supported offensive Barrages meet at the right distance and angle, they can enter a Barrage Clash instead of politely phasing through each other.

07 • BARRAGE GUARD

YES, YOU CAN PUNCH SOME PROJECTILES AWAY

SP / TW / attack-mode Crazy Diamond / Silver Chariot / King Crimson create a forward guard line during Barrage, roughly 2.5 tiles wide total.

The projectile has to genuinely cross that line from the front. Side and rear shots still hurt, and explosions, area fire or THREE FREEZE are not going to respect your dramatic punching.

08 • PVP / SAFETY

SAFETY PROTECTS HP. STANDS HAVE OTHER IDEAS.

Stand HP damage respects Project Zomboid PvP/Safety. Non-damaging utility and CC can have separate rules, including things such as THREE FREEZE, Space Pull and Epitaph.

If you're playing with friends, read your Stand page before discovering the rule by launching somebody across the room.

That's enough to start.
