[h2]HOW TO START[/h2]

Find a [b]Stand Arrow[/b] from world loot or rare zombie drops.

Use it to awaken a random Stand.

[b]1 Arrow = 3 successful uses[/b]

[b]3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → gone[/b]

Using another Arrow can give you a different Stand without deleting progress earned on your previous Stands.

Servers may also enable [b]Super Arrow[/b] to choose directly from currently loaded Stands.

[b]Super Arrow is OFF by default.[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]CONTROLS[/h2]

[b]CAPS LOCK[/b] — Summon / Dismiss Stand
[b]1–4[/b] — Stand Skills

Some abilities support Tap / Hold / Full Charge and may do a different move depending on how long you hold the key.

Personal options: [b]Options → Mods → Project JoJo[/b]

Targeting Modes:
• [b]Mouse / Pointer[/b] — attacks follow the cursor
• [b]Facing Direction[/b] — attacks follow the direction your character is facing
• [b]Controller Aim[/b] — attacks follow the controller aim vector

You can also customize Stand HUD mode, HUD scale, opacity, personal Project JoJo volume and keybinds.

[h2]CORE STANDS — QUICK STYLE GUIDE[/h2]

[b]STAR PLATINUM[/b] — Close-range fighter. Builds Critical chance through ORA hits; Critical attacks hit extremely hard and can launch enemies into walls/other targets. Has a 5-second Time Stop.

[b]THE WORLD[/b] — Heavy all-rounder. Strong melee, long-range knives and a 9-second Time Stop. Easy to understand, dangerous at almost any distance.

[b]KING CRIMSON[/b] — Counter / survival Stand. Use Epitaph when you expect a real attack, then punish from a better angle. Time Erase is excellent for escaping bad situations.

[b]CRAZY DIAMOND[/b] — Melee support. Fight to refill Restore, then spend it to heal allies, repair supported objects or use Glass Homing on a marked target.

[b]THE HAND[/b] — High-risk space control. Pull enemies, reposition and ERASE targets, but all of those tools compete for the same Space Gauge.

[b]KILLER QUEEN[/b] — Bomb setup / AOE. Turn a target or area into a bomb, choose when to detonate, or deploy Sheer Heart Attack to pressure enemies automatically.

[b]SILVER CHARIOT[/b] — Fast sword fighter. Movement refills Speed Gauge; spend it on Rush/Sweep and use Rapier Shot when you need range. Armor Off has its own cooldown and never prevents normal Stand Summon/Dismiss.

[b]MAGICIAN'S RED[/b] — Ranged fire and horde control. Cross Fire builds Fire Gauge on hit; the same skill can place Fire Trap. Spend Fire on Flame Spray / Cross Fire Bomb and use Fire Control / Detector to manage the battlefield.

[b]ECHOES ACT 3[/b] — Single-target control. Not a raw-damage Stand: pick the dangerous enemy and lock it with THREE FREEZE for 9 seconds.

Every Stand has a full Wiki page with exact mechanics and practical examples.

[h2]STAND LEVELS[/h2]

Every Stand has separate progression: [b]LV.1 → LV.100[/b]

Stand-caused zombie kills grant EXP. With Stand Levels enabled, LV.1 starts at [b]50% damage[/b] and LV.100 reaches [b]120% damage[/b]. Switching Stands does not erase previous Stand progression.

[h2]CORE SYSTEMS[/h2]

• Directional First-Contact — attacks use their real reach and hit the first valid body/contact on the attack line
• Time Stop / Time Erase
• Barrage Clash / Barrage Guard
• Stand Gauges and Stand-specific resources
• Tap / Hold / Full Charge abilities
• PvP / Safety integration
• Stand Levels
• Multiplayer synchronization
• World / vehicle collision safety

While a registered Stand is summoned, its owner receives [b]33% less NET damage[/b].

Full mechanics and examples are explained in the Wiki.

[h2]MULTIPLAYER[/h2]

[b]Singleplayer / Host / Multiplayer supported.[/b]

Multiple players may use the same Stand. Player HP damage respects Project Zomboid's [b]PvP / Safety rules[/b].
