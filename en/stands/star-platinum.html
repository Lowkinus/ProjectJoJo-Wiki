STAR PLATINUM

ROLE:
Brawler / Crit Combo / Close Range / Time Control

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want aggressive melee, a growing Crit threat and a short but very strong Time Stop.

SKILL 1 — ORA PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.36 sec • Range: about 2.75 tiles • Full charge: about 1.2 sec.

Star Platinum is currently the only Stand with the active 5-hit Punch combo. Continue punching within about 0.95 sec to advance the chain; hit 5 is the strongest.

Tap for pressure. Charge when you have room to commit to a heavier hit.

CRITICAL SYSTEM

Starts at 15%. Every real Punch/Barrage contact that does not Crit adds +1%, up to 50%.

A Crit deals x3.5 damage and resets the chance to 15%. Star Finger can use the stored chance, but a failed Finger does not build it.

SKILL 2 — ORA BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5 sec • Normal: 15 hits • Full charge: 25 hits with increased damage.

Barrage builds Crit on non-Crit contacts and can enter Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 — STAR FINGER

Cooldown: about 7 sec • Range: about 5 tiles • Full charge: about 1.05 sec.

A piercing line attack. Multiple targets can be hit if they are physically lined up in the Finger path. It can also use Star Platinum's stored Crit chance.

SKILL 4 — TIME DASH / TIME STOP

Cooldown: about 35 sec.

Tap: collision-aware Time Dash up to about 11.25 tiles (2.5× the previous range). Full charge: about 1 sec charge, then a 1.7 sec wind-up before a 5 sec Time Stop.

Damage dealt to supported frozen victims is held and released when time resumes. SP can also fully charge its own Time Stop from inside another Time Stop to gain its own movement window.
