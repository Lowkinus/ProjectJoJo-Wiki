START WITHOUT THE CONFUSION

These four things are enough to jump in. The long guide is there when you want the details.

STAND ARROW

Find a Stand Arrow

A normal Stand Arrow has 3 successful uses (3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → removed), and rolling a new Stand does not erase old Stand levels.

CAPS LOCK

Summon / Dismiss

CAPS LOCK summons or dismisses your current Stand.

1 – 4

Use Stand Skills

Use the top-row number keys. Many skills have a held/full-charge version.

MOUSE

Aim Attacks

Mouse direction controls your attack line. Close-range attacks have soft aim assistance.

!

One key can have two actions. KQ Skill 3 = First Bomb / Sheer Heart Attack; SP/TW Skill 4 = Time Dash / Time Stop.

WHAT YOU SHOULD KNOW

CORE SYSTEMS

STAND ARROW3 USES

Condition: 3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → removed • Infinite Arrow does not lose Condition.

STAND LEVELLV.1–100

Every Stand has separate progression. LV.1 starts near 50% and scales to full power.

TIMESTOP / ERASE

Star Platinum and The World use Time Stop; King Crimson uses a separate Time Erase system.

MULTIPLAYERPVP / SAFETY

Player damage respects PvP/Safety, and protected players are not autonomous SHA targets.
