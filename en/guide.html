PROJECT JOJO — COMPLETE STAND GUIDE

Current Guide Version: v0.23.0.232

Project Zomboid: Build 42.20+
Singleplayer / Host / Multiplayer

This is the detailed guide.

The Workshop description tells you WHAT the mod has.

This thread tells you:

"Okay, but how the hell do I actually use this Stand?"

Damage numbers are not listed everywhere because Stand damage changes with:

  • Stand Level
  • Sandbox Stand Damage
  • Critical Hits
  • Charge
  • Individual Stand mechanics

This guide focuses on what each skill actually DOES and how to use it effectively.

Getting Started↑ TOP

BASIC CONTROLS

CAPS LOCK — Summon / Dismiss Stand

1 — Skill 1
2 — Skill 2
3 — Skill 3
4 — Skill 4

These are the normal top-row number keys.

Not Numpad.

Your Stand follows your mouse direction when attacking.

The combat system includes soft aim assistance for close-range Stand attacks, so you do not need pixel-perfect mouse placement.

You still need to aim in the correct direction.

The Stand is helping you.

It is not playing the game for you.

Getting Started↑ TOP

CHARGING SKILLS

Many abilities have two meaningful states:

Normal Release
and
FULL CHARGE

Hold the skill until the full-charge cue.

Full charge may increase:

  • Damage
  • Number of Barrage hits
  • Range / radius
  • Duration
  • Healing
  • Impact
  • Or completely change what the skill does

Examples:

The Hand Skill 3
Tap = Space Dash
Full Charge = Space Pull

Killer Queen Skill 3
Tap = First Bomb
Full Charge = Sheer Heart Attack

Star Platinum / The World Skill 4
Tap = Time Dash
Full Charge = Time Stop

King Crimson Skill 4
Tap = Time Dash
Full Charge = Time Erase

So yes:

Holding buttons is important.

Sometimes holding a button for one second is the difference between:

"I moved forward."

and

"I stopped the concept of time."

STAND LEVEL SYSTEM

Every Stand has its own separate Level:

LV.1 → LV.100

At LV.1 your Stand starts at approximately:

50% of its full Stand damage

It gradually scales upward until:

LV.100 = 100% Stand power

Only zombies actually killed by your Stand give Stand EXP.

Normal weapon kills do not count.

Your progress is stored separately for every Stand.

Example:

Star Platinum — LV.73
The World — LV.41
Killer Queen — LV.18

Using another Stand Arrow does NOT delete the Levels you already earned.

If you later obtain the old Stand again, its progression is still there.

Some useful milestones:

LV.5 ≈ 20 Stand kills
LV.10 ≈ 51
LV.20 ≈ 131
LV.30 ≈ 236
LV.50 ≈ 521
LV.75 ≈ 1,018
LV.100 ≈ 1,671

Death resets Stand progression for that character.

If the Stand Level System is disabled in Sandbox:

Every Stand operates at full power.

Your saved real progression is preserved.

You did not grind 1,000 zombies just for me to throw your homework away.

Getting Started↑ TOP

STAND ARROW

Stand Arrows can appear in world loot and can rarely drop from zombies.

At the default 1x setting:

Zombie drop chance is approximately 1 / 250.

Tool / garage / mechanic-type storage is generally one of the better places to search.

General containers can contain Arrows too.

Vehicle loot is intentionally rarer.

A normal Stand Arrow now uses Project Zomboid's native item Condition.

1 Arrow = 3 successful uses.

The visible Condition goes:

3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → removed

Using an Arrow gives you a Stand from the available Stand pool.

Changing Stands does NOT erase the Levels you already earned on older Stands.

If Infinite Stand Arrow is enabled in Sandbox, the Arrow does not lose Condition when used.

So you can choose between rare survival progression or:

"Okay boys, tonight everyone gets a Stand."

Core Systems↑ TOP

MULTIPLAYER / PVP RULES

This part is IMPORTANT.

Project JoJo treats damage and Stand utility / crowd control as different things.

When Project Zomboid PvP / Safety protects a player:

HP / lethal damage is blocked.

Designed non-damaging utility can still work, including:

  • Time Stop
  • THREE FREEZE
  • Space Pull
  • Barrage Clash
  • Epitaph counter / reposition
  • First Bomb attachment
  • Other non-damaging Stand interactions

The Hand's Space Pull is specifically designed as utility.

Pulling another player NEVER deals HP damage to that player.

Use it to rescue a teammate from a horde, a bite attempt or a terrible decision.

The Hand ERASE is different.

If PvP / Safety protects the player:

ERASE cannot kill / delete that player.

SHEER HEART ATTACK — PVP EXCEPTION

SHA follows stricter autonomous-target rules.

If PvP / Safety protects another player:

  • SHA will NOT acquire that player as a target
  • If protection changes during bomb wind-up, SHA cancels that player target
  • SHA explosion splash skips protected players

If PvP is enabled and Safety allows the hit, the player can become a valid SHA target again.

First Bomb attachment remains a separate mechanic from SHA's autonomous target selection.

So:

"BRO I TURNED PVP OFF, WHY CAN YOU STILL PULL ME?"

Not a bug.

"BRO I TURNED PVP OFF, WHY IS SHA HUNTING ME?"

That WOULD be a bug on the current version.

BARRAGE CLASH

Supported offensive Barrages can collide with another player's Barrage.

Both users need to:

  • Be close enough
  • Face / aim toward each other
  • Actually be using an offensive Barrage

Maximum Clash distance is roughly:

5.25 tiles

While the Clash is happening:

The Barrages consume hits against each other instead of dealing normal HP/environment damage.

When one Barrage runs out first, the surviving Barrage can continue.

Crazy Diamond's Restore Barrage does NOT enter offensive Barrage Clash.

And yes:

Barrage Clash can still happen even when PvP damage is protected.

Your fists can have an argument without your health bars joining the conversation.

TIME STOP — IMPORTANT RULES

Star Platinum and The World have true Time Stop.

It affects much more than zombies.

The system can freeze supported:

  • Players
  • Zombies
  • Living actors
  • Movement
  • Attacks
  • Barrages
  • Delayed Stand actions
  • Grabs / lunges
  • Supported vehicles
  • Stand timers
  • Resource timers
  • Other supported moving objects

Damage dealt to a frozen victim is stored and released when time resumes.

So if someone is standing perfectly still while you punch them 15 times:

No.

The damage did not disappear.

Time is simply holding the receipt.

WHAT DOES THE VICTIM SEE?

A player without a Time Stop-capable Stand gets the Time Stop blackout.

A player who owns:

Star Platinum
or
The World

can perceive stopped time with the alternate gray Time Stop view.

However:

Seeing stopped time does NOT automatically mean you can move inside it.

You remain frozen until your own Time Stop activates.

COUNTERING ANOTHER TIME STOP

If Star Platinum or The World is frozen inside another Time Stop:

They can still fully charge their own Time Stop.

When their Time Stop activates, they gain their own movement window.

Example:

Player A stops time for 9 seconds.

Player B activates a 5-second Star Platinum Time Stop inside it.

Player B receives their own 5-second movement window.

When those 5 seconds end:

If Player A's original Time Stop is still active...

Player B freezes again.

Anime logic.

Implemented literally.

MANUAL TIME RESUME

If your own Time Stop is active:

Press the same Time Stop skill again to end it early.

Useful when:

  • You already finished attacking
  • You need the cooldown to begin
  • You do not want to stare dramatically at a frozen zombie for the remaining three seconds
Core Systems↑ TOP

TIME STOP VS TIME ERASE

King Crimson uses a different temporal system.

Time Stop and Time Erase cannot simply coexist normally.

If a Time Stop is already active or being declared:

King Crimson cannot start a new full Time Erase.

If Time Erase existed FIRST and someone later activates Time Stop:

The systems compare remaining temporal duration.

If the Time Stop duration is shorter than the remaining Time Erase:

Time Erase wins.

If the Time Stop has enough duration to cancel the remaining Time Erase:

Time Erase ends and the leftover Time Stop duration continues.

If they consume each other exactly:

Mutual cancel.

Basically:

The game performs temporal accounting.

Diavolo's greatest enemy was apparently mathematics.

Medium

Best For:
Players who want aggressive melee, combos, high burst and Time Stop.

SKILL 1 — ORA PUNCH

Cooldown: approximately 0.36 sec
Range: approximately 2.75 tiles
Full Charge: approximately 1.2 sec

Star Platinum has a unique 5-stage Punch combo.

Keep punching within roughly:

0.95 seconds

to continue the combo chain.

The fifth Punch is the strongest hit.

Full charging the Punch dramatically increases its damage and impact.

HOW TO USE IT:

Normal Punches are excellent when something is already in your face.

Do not full-charge every single Punch.

Against one normal zombie:

Fast repeated punches are usually more practical.

Use the full charge when:

  • A dangerous target is approaching
  • You have distance
  • Time is stopped
  • You want a heavy opening hit
  • You simply need that zombie to reconsider its career

Cooldown: approximately 5 sec

Normal:
15 hits

Full Charge:
25 hits + increased damage

The Barrage continuously follows your aim.

It can participate in Barrage Clash.

TIP:

The Stand has aim assistance, but Barrage is not a turret.

SKILL 3 — STAR FINGER

Cooldown: approximately 7 sec
Range: approximately 5 tiles
Full Charge: approximately 1.05 sec

Star Finger extends forward along the mouse direction.

It is a piercing line attack.

Enemies lined up along the finger's path can be hit instead of requiring the cursor to sit perfectly on one exact zombie.

BEST USE:

Line up several zombies in a corridor.

Congratulations.

You have invented zombie kebab.

Cooldown: approximately 35 sec

Tap:
Time Dash — approximately 4.5 tiles

Full Charge:
Time Stop

Wind-up after activation:
approximately 1.7 sec

Time Stop duration:
5 seconds

Time Dash is collision-aware.

It will not intentionally teleport you through solid walls, closed doors or invalid terrain.

TIP:

Do not think of Skill 4 as "the Time Stop button."

Half of its value is the Dash.

A 35-second Time Stop cooldown does NOT mean you should ignore a 4.5-tile emergency teleport.

STAR PLATINUM CRITICAL SYSTEM

Base Crit:
15%

Every Punch/Barrage contact that does NOT Crit:

+1 percentage point

Maximum:
50%

When Star Platinum finally Crits:

x3.5 damage

Then the Crit chance resets to 15%.

Star Finger can use the stored Crit chance.

However:

Star Finger does NOT build the Crit meter when it fails to Crit.

Punch and Barrage are your Crit builders.

PLAYSTYLE TIP:

If your Crit meter is high, consider saving it for:

  • A charged Punch
  • A heavy finisher
  • Star Finger
  • A Time Stop attack window

Do not waste a 48% Crit on the zombie already missing both arms unless that zombie personally offended you.

Maximum HP:

150

SKILL 4 — GLASS SHOT / GLASS HOMING

ATTACK MODE — GLASS SHOT

Cooldown:
approximately 5.2 sec

Fires Glass normally in the direction of your aim.

Where the Glass ends up becomes your latest:

GLASS MARK

The Mark can be:

  • A living target
  • A player
  • A zombie
  • The ground
  • A wall / door / object
  • The final impact position of the Glass

If Glass Shot hits a living target, the Mark follows that target.

If it does not hit a living target, Crazy Diamond stores the impact position as a fixed World Mark.

Only one Mark can exist at a time.

A new Glass Shot replaces the previous Mark.

GLASS MARK TIMER

A Mark lasts:

60 seconds

If no new Skill 4 use refreshes it, the Mark disappears.

Successfully firing either:

Glass Shot
or
Glass Homing

refreshes the active Mark timer back to:

60 seconds

Glass Shot can also replace it with a new target or impact point.

If a Living Mark dies:

The Mark is immediately lost.

Corpses cannot remain Homing targets.

RESTORE MODE — GLASS HOMING

Cooldown:
approximately 7 sec

Requires an active Glass Mark.

No Mark:

No Glass Homing.

The projectile launches in the direction you are aiming first, then slowly curves toward the Mark.

Glass Homing is intentionally slow.

This gives enemies time to see it coming and lets players react by moving behind cover.

The projectile still physically travels through the world.

If something crosses its path first:

  • Zombie
  • Player
  • Other valid living target
  • Wall
  • Door
  • Window
  • Vehicle
  • Supported obstacle

The Glass hits that first and stops.

It does NOT ignore enemies or walls just because something else is Marked.

A World Mark can therefore be used tactically.

Example:

Mark the ground near a doorway
→ switch to Restore Mode
→ fire Glass Homing
→ a zombie walks across the projectile's path
→ the zombie gets hit instead

You are not only marking enemies.

You can mark where you expect an enemy to be.

Glass Homing uses a distinctive golden effect.

TIP:

A Living Mark is for chasing a target.

A World Mark is for planning ahead.

Sometimes the best target is not where the zombie is.

It is where the zombie is about to be.

CRAZY DIAMOND TEAM TIP

Do NOT waste all your Restore Gauge healing tiny scratches after every fight.

Fight in Attack Mode.

Build Gauge.

Use Glass Shot to mark important targets.

Save Restore for:

  • Serious injuries
  • Team emergencies
  • Vehicle damage
  • Important equipment/world repair
  • A Homing Glass follow-up when you already have a useful target marked

Crazy Diamond becomes much stronger when the player treats Restore Gauge, Mode switching and the Glass Mark as resources instead of free infinite healing.

SKILL 3 — EPITAPH

Cooldown:
approximately 12 sec

Normal duration:
5 seconds

Full Charge:
approximately 1.2 sec

Full duration:
approximately 8 seconds

Epitaph arms a one-hit counter window.

It does NOT teleport just because something walked near you.

The attacker must actually commit a dangerous attack.

Supported counter paths include:

  • Zombie attacks
  • Stand attacks
  • Player weapon attack attempts

When triggered:

  • The dangerous hit is negated
  • Your protected body state is restored
  • You can be repositioned behind the attacker when a valid square exists
  • Epitaph is consumed

It can protect against nasty consequences associated with that countered hit.

IMPORTANT:

It protects against ONE real hit.

Not:

"I pressed Skill 3, therefore I am immortal for eight seconds."

HOW TO USE EPITAPH

Excellent before:

  • Opening a dangerous door
  • Moving through a tight zombie group
  • Entering melee against another player
  • Fighting when one bite could end the character
  • Doing something your brain has correctly identified as a bad idea

Epitaph is insurance.

Unlike real insurance:

It actually reacts immediately.

SKILL 4 FULL — TIME ERASE

Cooldown:
approximately 32 sec

Full Charge:
approximately 1.3 sec

Duration:
10 seconds

During Time Erase the user becomes temporarily:

  • Protected from normal damage
  • Intangible to normal Stand targeting
  • Hidden from normal enemy attention
  • Visually absent to other observers
  • Free to move

A decoy remains behind for the outside world.

Enemies can interact with the false presence instead of the real user.

Your normal skill use is locked while Time Erase is active.

This is primarily:

Survival / repositioning / aggro break

not:

"10 seconds of invulnerability while I spam Barrage."

Press Skill 4 again while Time Erase is active:

Exit early.

KING CRIMSON PLAYSTYLE

Use Epitaph when you expect one dangerous hit.

Use Time Dash when you only need movement.

Use Time Erase when the entire situation has become unsalvageable.

In other words:

Star Platinum solves problems.

The World dominates problems.

King Crimson waits for the problem to happen and then claims that part of history never existed.

FINAL ADVICE

There is no single intended way to play every Stand.

Star Platinum rewards pressure and Crit building.

The World rewards burst and Time Stop management.

Silver Chariot rewards movement.

Killer Queen rewards preparation.

Echoes rewards target priority.

The Hand rewards positioning and creativity.

Magician's Red rewards crowd and resource management.

Crazy Diamond rewards teamwork and resource cycling.

King Crimson rewards timing and survival instincts.

If you only look at:

"Which Stand has the biggest damage number?"

you are ignoring half of the mod.

A Stand is not just a weapon.

It is a playstyle.

And sometimes that playstyle is:

"My friend is about to die, so I will erase the distance between us and violently drag him twenty tiles across the street."

Perfectly normal co-op behavior.
