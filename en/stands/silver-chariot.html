SILVER CHARIOT

A movement-driven sword Stand. Speed Gauge refills much faster while running/sprinting and is spent on Rush and Sweep, so mobility is part of your resource engine.

Current guide refresh • Core v0.23.0.280
DifficultyMedium
Speed max100
Rush cost18
Sweep cost25

SPEED GAUGE

Idle 0.1/s • Walk 1/s • Run 11/s • Sprint 20/s

Standing still barely refills Speed. Real movement is how you recharge quickly.

Example: After spending Speed on a Sweep, sprint a short safe loop instead of standing still waiting for the bar.

Skill 1 — STAB

CD 0.22s • Range 3.1

Fast single-target sword poke for reliable damage without spending Speed Gauge.

Example: Use Stab while rebuilding Gauge; save the expensive skills for when movement has refilled you.

Skill 2 — RAPIER RUSH

CD 3s • Range 3.4 • Cost 18

Rapid sword rush. Strong for focused pressure but spends Speed, so repeated use requires movement between engagements.

Example: Rush one target, disengage and run, then come back with the Gauge you earned from movement.

Skill 3 — CIRCLE SWEEP

CD 3.5s • Area • Cost 25

Wide sword sweep for multiple nearby threats. It costs more Speed than Rush.

Example: Use it when the group is actually surrounding you; using 25 Speed on one lone zombie wastes the Stand’s resource.

Skill 4 — RAPIER SHOT

CD 5.2s • Range 8.2

Launches one real rapier projectile. It gives Silver Chariot a ranged answer and can be stopped by supported Barrage Guard.

Example: Shoot a dangerous target before it enters melee range, then use your movement speed to choose whether to close in or back off.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT THIS STAND

Do not memorize jargon

Focus on the loop: what builds your resource, what spends it, and which skill solves the problem in front of you.

Use the examples

The examples above are not fixed combos. They are situations that explain why the mechanic exists, so you can adapt it to your own fight.
