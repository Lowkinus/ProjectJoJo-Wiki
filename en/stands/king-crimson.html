KING CRIMSON

v0.23.0.237

Punch/Barrage use First-Contact and King Crimson's Barrage can activate the forward projectile guard.

Skill 1 — PUNCH

Heavy melee • about 2.9 tiles.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

17 normal hits / 26 full-charge hits • supports Barrage Clash.

Skill 3 — EPITAPH

Counters the first real valid attack during the window and attempts to reposition behind the attacker.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / TIME ERASE

Tap: Time Dash up to about 13.75 tiles • full charge: 10 sec Time Erase.

Aiming in this version

Attacks covered by Directional First-Contact use mouse direction and the skill's real range. The first body intersecting the attack corridor is selected first, while existing obstacles and PvP/Safety rules still apply.
