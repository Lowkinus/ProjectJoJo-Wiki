[h2]HOW HEAVEN'S DOOR WORKS[/h2]

[b]1 — OPEN THE BOOK[/b]

Build Book hits with Heaven's Door contact, use PAGE OPEN BARRAGE, PAGE TOUCH, or let BOOK TRAP open targets for you.

[b]2 — WRITE THE ORDER[/b]

Choose a command the target can actually perform. Orders may execute immediately, after a delay, or when a supported target enters the trigger range.

[b]3 — READ THE RESULT[/b]

A green message above you confirms when an order actually succeeds. Timed effects show a red message when they end or are cancelled.

A successful written command consumes the currently opened book, so open the target again before writing the next command.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]REWRITE — 0 / 100[/h2]

REWRITE is designed as a [b]large combat reserve[/b], not a fast passive mana bar.

Most commands are cheap, so a full gauge can support a long chain of orders.

After a successful write, normal recovery pauses for [b]15 seconds[/b], then recovers very slowly at [b]0.02 / sec[/b]. Writing again restarts the wait.

[b]READING A REAL OPEN BOOK = 6.0 / sec[/b]

You do not need to finish reading anything. Keep the valid Book UI open and REWRITE begins recovering quickly immediately.

Opening the Book does [b]not[/b] instantly refill the gauge.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]SKILL 1 — BARRAGE[/h2]

Fast close-range rush that deals light damage and contributes to shared Book progress.

It helps prepare the target, but does not open the Book by itself.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]SKILL 2 — PAGE OPEN BARRAGE[/h2]

A lighter rush focused on opening the target's Book through repeated physical contact.

Once shared Book progress reaches the requirement, this route opens the target.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]SKILL 3 — PAGE TOUCH / WRITE MYSELF[/h2]

[b]Tap[/b] — zero-damage physical touch that immediately opens a valid target on real contact.

[b]Full charge[/b] — open your own Book for self-buffs, knowledge and self-directed orders.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]SKILL 4 — BOOK TRAP[/h2]

Place one persistent Book Trap at range.

The trap pulses in an area and has a shared budget of [b]10 Book hits[/b]. Each valid target hit consumes one point from that budget.

If seven valid targets are inside one pulse, seven hits are spent at once. When the budget is exhausted, the trap disappears and a new one can be placed.

Hold Skill 4 to collect your existing trap.
