DIRECTIONAL FIRST-CONTACT AIM

In v0.23.0.237, the mouse defines direction while hit checks use the skill's true maximum range, so a nearby cursor no longer shortens a long-range skill.

  • The first body intersecting the attack corridor is selected first.
  • Standing, fallen and crawling targets use appropriate contact capsules.
  • Barrage follows mouse direction and can retain the previous contact for ~240 ms if no new target is found.
  • 0.30-tile side tolerance; a new real contact wins immediately.
  • Turning away, leaving range, death or walls/doors/windows/vehicles breaking the path ends contact.

BARRAGE GUARD / PROJECTILE DEFLECTION

Enabled for Star Platinum, The World, Crazy Diamond, Silver Chariot and King Crimson.

  • Total guard width is about 2.5 tiles.
  • The projectile must come from the front and genuinely cross the guard line.
  • Direct support: TW Knife, SC Rapier Shot, CD Glass / Glass Homing.
  • Crazy Diamond Restore Barrage does not activate Guard.
  • Does not block explosions, area fire, area erasure or THREE FREEZE.
Vanilla firearms: a native per-hit hook exists, but live B42 validation across SP / Host / Dedicated is still pending. If the hook is unavailable, firearm damage passes through normally.
Third-party projectiles: must integrate JJS_BarrageGuard.interceptProjectile(attacker,sx,sy,ex,ey,z).

TIME STOP / TIME ERASE

Star Platinum stops time for 5 sec, The World for 9 sec, while King Crimson uses a 10 sec Time Erase. If Erase is already active, an incoming Time Stop spends its duration against the remaining Erase time; a longer Stop cancels Erase and continues with leftover Stop time.

MULTIPLAYER / PVP / SAFETY

Player HP damage continues to respect the existing PvP/Safety rules. Utility/CC designed not to deal HP damage follows its own rules. Barrage Guard does not use GodMode, health rollback or random world-object deletion.
