THE WORLD

ROLE:
Heavy Burst / Range / Time Duelist

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want an all-round combat Stand with the longest normal Time Stop.

SKILL 1 — MUDA PUNCH

Cooldown: approximately 0.39 sec
Full Charge: approximately 1.2 sec

A very heavy single-target Punch.

Full charge greatly increases the hit.

Unlike Star Platinum's Skill 1, The World's current Punch does NOT use Star Platinum's active 5-stage combo mechanic.

It hits hard.

It does not need a PowerPoint presentation.

SKILL 2 — MUDA BARRAGE

Cooldown: approximately 5.2 sec

Normal:
15 hits

Full:
28 hits

Excellent sustained damage and one of The World's main burst tools.

Can Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 — KNIFE THROW

Cooldown: approximately 1.6 sec
Range: approximately 9 tiles

Full Charge:
approximately 0.9 sec

The World's ranged pressure tool.

Very useful for:

  • Finishing weakened enemies
  • Fighting without entering bite range
  • Pulling attention
  • Doing something while your big cooldowns recover

If a vampire throwing kitchen equipment at people sounds stupid:

It worked in the anime.

SKILL 4 — TIME DASH / ZA WARUDO

Cooldown: approximately 42 sec

Tap:
Time Dash — approximately 5.25 tiles

Full:
Time Stop

Wind-up:
approximately 0.75 sec

Duration:
9 seconds

The World gets a much longer stopped-time attack window than Star Platinum.

That is extremely powerful.

That is also why the cooldown is not five seconds.

GOOD COMBO:

ZA WARUDO
→ reposition
→ Barrage
→ Knife / Punch
→ move somewhere safe
→ resume time

Your opponent:

"Why am I over here?"

Also your opponent:

"Why is my health missing?"
