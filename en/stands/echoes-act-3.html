ECHOES ACT 3

ROLE:
Single-Target Crowd Control / Team Setup

Difficulty:
Easy to understand, tactical to master

Best For:
Teams, dangerous single targets and players who enjoy telling physics to stop.

SKILL 1 — BARRAGE + MARK

Cooldown:
approximately 4.7 sec

Normal:
14 hits

Full:
23 hits

Damage is intentionally low.

That is not the main purpose.

A successfully hit target becomes the Barrage target for the automatic Freeze flow.

When the Barrage finishes:

THREE FREEZE is automatically applied to that target.

This is extremely useful because you can:

Damage
→ immediately control

instead of spending another manual action.

PvP NOTE:

If PvP/Safety blocks the actual player damage, the automatic Barrage mark does not count as a damaging hit.

For a protected teammate/player:

Use Skill 2 directly if you actually want to Freeze them.

SKILL 2 — THREE FREEZE

Cooldown:
9 seconds

Freeze duration:
9 seconds

Range:
approximately 9 tiles

Instant activation.

No direct damage.

Only one target per Echoes owner can remain controlled.

Freeze a new target:

The previous target is released.

THREE FREEZE also supports targeted vehicle freezing.

Yes.

ACT 3 can look at a vehicle and basically say:

"Park."

TEAM USE

Echoes is much stronger when someone understands what the Stand is for.

Do NOT look at its raw DPS and compare it to The World.

Use THREE FREEZE on:

  • The dangerous zombie reaching your injured friend
  • A dangerous NPC / supported living target
  • A player you need to stop
  • A vehicle
  • A single threat your team needs time to deal with

Example:

Friend is reloading.

Zombie is approaching.

THREE FREEZE.

Friend reloads.

Problem solved.

No 9-second MUDA monologue required.
