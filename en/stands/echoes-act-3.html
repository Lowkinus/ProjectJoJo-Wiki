ECHOES ACT 3

ROLE:
Single-Target CC / Team Setup

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want to stop the most dangerous target in the room rather than compete for raw DPS.

SKILL 1 — BARRAGE → THREE FREEZE

Cooldown: about 4.7 sec • Range: 2.8 tiles • Normal: 14 hits • Full charge: 23 hits.

The Barrage remembers the last real target it actually hit. When the Barrage ends, THREE FREEZE is automatically applied to that target if it is still valid.

That turns Skill 1 into a close-range combo that naturally ends in crowd control.

SKILL 2 — THREE FREEZE

Cooldown: about 9 sec • Range: about 9 tiles • Freeze duration: 9 sec.

Deals no direct damage. It can freeze a targeted living actor immediately, and targeted vehicles can also be frozen for 9 sec.

One Echoes owner controls one main target at a time. Freezing a new target releases the old one, and invalid/out-of-range/different-floor targets are cleared.

PvP/Safety protects HP damage, but does not automatically block this kind of non-damaging crowd control.

TEAM USE

Freeze the zombie about to bite a teammate, the player you need to stop, or a vehicle that needs to stay put.

Echoes is strongest when you use it to remove the most urgent threat from the fight for a few seconds.
