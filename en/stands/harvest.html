HARVEST

HARVEST uses one shared colony for combat and Search. The main skill is resource management: every unit sent away is one less unit available until it returns.

Current addon guide • v0.4.21
HOW TO GET HARVEST

Use the normal Project JoJo Stand Arrow. HARVEST joins the Stand pool while this addon is enabled, and appears in Super Arrow selection if that Sandbox option is on.

CONTROLS

Caps Lock — Summon/Dismiss
1 — HARVEST SHOT
2 — NEEDLE SWARM
3 — HARVEST SEARCH COMMAND
4 — HARVEST STORM

COLONY — 0 / 500

Combat and Search use one shared pool of up to 500 Harvest. Units away on Search stay reserved and lower your regeneration ceiling until they return or are recalled. Base recovery: Still 0.80/s, Seated/Vehicle 4.05/s, Rest 9.00/s, Sleep 14.40/s.

SKILL 1 — HARVEST SHOT

Costs 1 Harvest. Fast ranged harassment with light damage/mini knockback. Hold to keep sending one unit at a time. Supported Barrage Guard can defend against it.

SKILL 2 — NEEDLE SWARM

Minimum 7 Harvest. Hold to assign more. The committed swarm attaches to one target and strikes together after a delay. Invalid/dead targets refund the reserved units.

SKILL 3 — SEARCH COMMAND

Choose an item already in your inventory as a template, requested quantity and assigned crew. Quantity x1–x500. x1–x20 requires at least 20 Harvest; x21+ requires at least the requested quantity. More crew speeds work; total batch weight adds transport time. Active jobs can be individually RECALLed.

SKILL 4 — HARVEST STORM

Minimum 33 Harvest. Release a committed portion of the colony around yourself for repeated area pressure. Larger commitments last longer, up to the tuned cap. Barrage Guard can protect the guarding player from Storm contacts without cancelling the entire Storm.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

If 150 Harvest are away on Search, your effective available ceiling is 350 until those units return. Starting a huge Storm at that moment can leave you nearly empty.
