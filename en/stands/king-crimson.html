KING CRIMSON

ROLE:
Counter / Survival / Escape / Temporal Control

Difficulty:
High

Best For:
Players who prefer reading the next dangerous moment, countering it, then using Time Erase to escape or reposition.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH

Cooldown: about 0.4 sec • Range: 2.9 tiles • Full charge: about 1.2 sec.

Straightforward heavy melee for when you need direct pressure.

SKILL 2 — BARRAGE

Cooldown: about 5.3 sec • Normal: 17 hits • Full charge: 26 hits.

Strong close pressure and supported Barrage Clash.

SKILL 3 — EPITAPH COUNTER

Cooldown: about 12 sec • Normal counter window: 5 sec • Full charge ~1.2 sec → about 8 sec.

Epitaph protects against the first real valid attack during the window and attempts to reposition King Crimson behind the attacker when a valid square exists.

It no longer triggers just because an enemy walked nearby; an actual committed attack has to happen. Existing wounds from before activation remain, so this is not a free heal.

Committed player attacks can still trigger the non-damaging counter/reposition even when PvP/Safety separately blocks HP damage.

SKILL 4 TAP — TIME DASH

Tap for a collision-aware dash of about 5.5 tiles. Use it for quick repositioning without committing to full Time Erase.

SKILL 4 FULL — TIME ERASE

Cooldown: about 32 sec • Full charge: about 1.3 sec • Duration: 10 sec.

During Time Erase the real user is protected from normal damage/targeting and can move, while normal Stand skill use is locked. A decoy remains for the outside world.

Press Skill 4 again to exit early.

Time Stop vs Time Erase: if Time Stop is already active/pending, a new full Time Erase cannot start. If Erase was active first, an incoming Time Stop spends its duration against the remaining Erase time: shorter Stop is consumed, longer Stop cancels Erase and continues with leftover time, and an exact match cancels both.
