Skill 1 — PUNCH

0.44s CD2.80 range0.12 damage1.15s full charge

The punch follows your aim and must physically reach a target. It can lightly assist targeting around 1.35 tiles and begins checking for a hit about 0.155 seconds after the attack starts. Full charge multiplies damage by ×1.85 and impact by ×1.45.

Full-charge raw damage: 0.12 × 1.85 = 0.222 before level, server, PvP and critical modifiers.

Skill 2 — BARRAGE

5.1s CD3.10 range0.034 / hit14 hits1.65s full charge

The Barrage follows your mouse while active. Hits are spaced about 0.14 seconds apart and the rush begins about 0.12 seconds after startup.

Normal: 14 × 0.034 = 0.476 approximate maximum total.

Full charge: 22 hits with ×1.18 damage per hit = 0.04012 each, about 0.88264 approximate maximum total before Level / Sandbox / PvP / Crit.

Skill 3 — MIND READ / YES–NO

0s CD10s durationCosts 1 Mind Point1.0s full charge

Tap / release before FULL = DODGE READ. This is the original defensive read: it lasts 10 seconds, costs 1 Mind Point and can be consumed once by a supported incoming attack. The attacker is sidestepped near their own position; distant shooters are never pulled toward ATUM. This mode does not enable the Skill 4 prediction combo.

Hold until FULL = GAMBLE READ. It also costs 1 point and lasts 10 seconds, but incoming monster/player attacks cannot consume it. It is consumed only when Skill 4 makes real contact, enabling the YES / NO prediction phase for the wager.

Only one read mode can be armed at a time. If either mode is active, pressing Skill 3 again—tap or hold—only cancels the current mode. Press Skill 3 once more to arm the other mode; there is no one-press hot-swap.

Manual cancel refund: cancelling an unused active read before timeout refunds +1 Mind Point (up to 100). No refund is given after DODGE READ triggers, after GAMBLE READ is consumed by Skill 4, or after the 10-second timer expires.

Skill 4 Passive interaction: while AUTO GAMBLE PASSIVE is armed, a successful tap-S3 DODGE READ automatically challenges the exact attacker that triggered the dodge. This route does not grant YES/NO prediction; prediction remains exclusive to FULL-charge S3.

MIND GAUGE

Starts 0Maximum 100S3 costs 1Game win +0 to +3

After ATUM wins Skill 4 and the result resolves, the owner rolls 0–3 Mind Points. Manually cancelling an unused active Skill 3 read before timeout refunds the 1 point spent to arm it.

If FULL-charge GAMBLE READ → S4 predicts correctly and ATUM still wins, the normal wager reward is guaranteed to be at least +1.

Skill 4 — GAMBLE / AUTO GAMBLE PASSIVE

Tap: 3s CD2.80 range0 contact damage1.0s FULL hold

Tap while Passive is OFF: ATUM lunges with Skill 1's 2.80-tile reach. The touch deals no damage and starts the wager only after real contact, choosing Rock Paper Scissors / Baseball / Racing with equal 1/3 odds.

Hold to FULL: toggles AUTO GAMBLE PASSIVE without spending Mind Gauge and without throwing the invite punch. Hold to FULL again to turn the Passive OFF.

While Passive is ON: tap-S4 is locked and will not lunge. You must FULL-hold S4 to turn Passive OFF before ordinary tap-S4 works again. The Passive stays armed after it triggers until you disable it.

FULL S4 + tap S3: a successful DODGE READ automatically uses the exact attacker as the Gamble target. FULL S3 + tap S4 remains the YES/NO prediction combo.

Once a minigame is already open, Skill 4 still cannot cancel it mid-session.

SOUL EXTRACTION — WHAT HAPPENS WHEN ATUM WINS?

Zombie / NPC loser

The target receives ATUM's 9999-damage finisher . In Stand terms, this is ATUM taking the loser's soul.

Player with PvP allowed

If Project JoJo's PvP / Safety rules allow HP damage, the losing player receives the same finisher and dies.

Safety-protected player

ATUM does not bypass PvP rules. If HP damage is blocked, the losing player is not killed and receives a 3-second blackout instead.

If the ATUM owner loses

The owner is not killed by their own finisher. All four ATUM skills are silenced/locked for 7 seconds, and resolution applies a 3-second blackout.

What happens after the game ends

Show the win/loss result for about 0.5s → close the game UI → keep protection for 0.5s → restore the player to the world → 0.5s later resolve the loser / Soul Finisher → if ATUM won, play Finish Point and roll +0 to +3 Mind Gauge.

ROCK PAPER SCISSORS

Choice time

The challenged side gets 8 seconds to choose Rock / Paper / Scissors. Zombies use a server-side random choice.

ATUM owner timer

The ATUM owner also sees an 8-second countdown, but that display does not automatically make the owner lose when it reaches zero.

FULL S3 → S4

The 5-second prediction phase only appears when Skill 3 was fully charged into GAMBLE READ. YES reveals the real choice; NO at minimum proves the guessed option is not the answer. Tap S3 is DODGE READ and does not enter prediction.

Tie

A tie resolves without Soul Finisher and without a win reward.

BASEBALL

Intro

About 6.6 seconds of opening presentation before the real decision timer starts.

HIGH / MIDDLE / LOW

The real choice window is 8 seconds. Match the target's hidden lane to hit and win. Choose the wrong lane and ATUM loses.

HOME RUN

A correct hit has a normal 10% Home Run chance. A correct swing in the final 2 seconds guarantees Home Run.

What it means in play

Hit and Home Run are both ATUM wins for Soul Extraction / Gauge. Home Run is a special result presentation, not a separate extra Gauge roll.

RACING

Intro8.8s
Ready4.5s
Main race36s
Vehicle HP100
Base speed5.7
1 — BOOST

Hold with no cooldown. HP drain ramps from about 2.2 → 4.6 HP/s over 2.2 seconds. Your speed scales from ×1.28 to ×1.95 while the non-boosting opponent loses relative pace. If both boost together, the relative advantage cancels while both still pay HP.

Hidden Launch Boost

During Ready, hit BOOST 5 times inside a 1.4-second window to arm a free 2-second launch boost. A correct S3 car prediction adds another +2 seconds.

2 — ATTACK

1.5-second warning charge → 260 ms dash → 350 ms return. Cooldown 4.8s. A normal hit deals 16 vehicle damage, knocks about 0.55 distance off the opponent and applies a 1.1-second ×0.78 slow.

ATTACK vs BLOCK

A blocked Attack deals 12 counter damage to the attacker. Two Attacks colliding deal 8 HP to each car.

3 — BLOCK

Costs 4 HP to activate a 0.75-second 100% protection window. Cooldown 5s.

Normal car contact

Mouse steering overlap deals no HP damage. The cars are separated; real damage comes from ATTACK / counter / HP costs / leaving the road.

Immediate loss

Vehicle HP reaching 0 is an immediate loss. Leaving the road during the steerable phase sets HP to 0 and loses the race.

Final Stretch

The finish road cannot appear early: the main road must run the full 36 seconds. A 0.76-second fade preserves the exact lead gap. The line is placed another 26 distance units beyond the leader. Steering locks, but BOOST / ATTACK / BLOCK remain active until somebody crosses.

AI Difficulty

EASY 10% / MEDIUM 20% / HARD 30% / HARDCORE 40%. Higher difficulty manages HP and skill timing more aggressively.

DAMAGE / LEVEL SCALING

Damage values shown above are the skill's base values. Project JoJo then applies Stand Level damage scaling from 50% at Lv.1 → 100% at Lv.100 linearly. Disabling Stand Level behaves as max level. Sandbox Stand Damage, PvP scaling and criticals can modify the final applied damage further.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Do not waste Mind Points

S3 has no cooldown, but the point is still a resource. Cancel an unused read before timeout to recover the point; triggered or expired reads are not refunded.

FULL S3 → TAP S4

Fully charge S3 into GAMBLE READ → tap S4 and make real contact → use YES/NO prediction → win → Soul Extraction → roll 0–3 Gauge back.

FULL S4 → TAP S3

FULL-hold S4 to arm AUTO GAMBLE PASSIVE → tap S3 for DODGE READ → if the dodge triggers, the attacker is challenged automatically without needing a tap-S4 reaction.

Baseball rewards nerve

Correct is more important than late, but if you are confident, landing the correct swing inside the final 2 seconds guarantees Home Run.

Racing is HP economy

BOOST uses HP as fuel. Over-spending it leaves you one clean ATTACK away from losing the entire wager.

Remember: Skill 4 is a real wager. If ATUM wins, a valid hostile loser is Soul-Finished. If ATUM loses, the owner loses access to all four skills for 7 seconds.
