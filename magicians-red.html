SKILLS & MECHANICS

FIRE GAUGE

Maximum 100

Cross Fire costs no Fire and adds 4 Fire on a valid damaging hit. Flame Spray uses 20; Cross Fire Bomb uses 32–55 based on charge.

Skill 1 — CROSS FIRE / FIRE TRAP

Cross Fire CD 1.7s · Range 8

Tap for a ranged Cross Fire shot; hold for Fire Trap on the same key. These are alternate actions, not two buttons at once.

Skill 2 — FLAME SPRAY

CD 5.2s · Range 5.2 · Fire 20

Sprays flame along a lane; charging changes the hit pattern.

Skill 3 — FIRE CONTROL / DETECTOR

CD 4.4s · Range 10

Fire Control manages/extinguishes fire; hold for Detector, a distinct function on the same skill slot.

Skill 4 — CROSS FIRE BOMB

CD 12s · Range 8 · Fire 32–55

Area fire attack. Full charge spends extra Fire for larger impact.

HUD SKILL NAMES

Double-action slots display compact SKILL/SKILL text, for example CROSS FIRE/FIRE TRAP and FIRE CONTROL/DETECTOR.
