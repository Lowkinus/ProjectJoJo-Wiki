[h2]HOW TO START[/h2]

Find a [b]Stand Arrow[/b] from world loot or rare zombie drops.
Use it to awaken a random Stand.
[b]1 Arrow = 3 successful uses[/b]
[b]3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → gone[/b]

Using another Arrow can grant a different Stand without deleting progress earned on previous Stands. Servers can optionally enable [b]Super Arrow[/b] to choose from currently registered Stands. [b]Super Arrow is OFF by default.[/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]CONTROLS[/h2]

[b]CAPS LOCK[/b] — Summon / Dismiss Stand
[b]1–4[/b] — Stand Skills

Some skill slots use Tap / Hold / Full Charge for different moves.
Personal settings: [b]Options → Mods → Project JoJo[/b]

Choose [b]Mouse / Pointer[/b], [b]Facing Direction[/b] or [b]Controller Aim[/b]. Personal HUD layout, scale, opacity, volume and keybinds can also be customized.

[h2]CORE STANDS — QUICK STYLE GUIDE[/h2]

[b]STAR PLATINUM[/b] — Close-range brawler. Builds critical chance with ORA hits, launches enemies on criticals, and stops time for 5 seconds.
[b]THE WORLD[/b] — Powerful melee, rapid Barrage, throwing knives and a 9-second Time Stop.
[b]KING CRIMSON[/b] — Counter / survival. Epitaph reacts to committed attacks, Time Erase repositions, and striking a target from behind offers a [b]77% critical chance[/b] against that target.
[b]CRAZY DIAMOND[/b] — Melee support. Build Restore to heal allies, repair supported objects and use Glass Homing.
[b]THE HAND[/b] — Pull and reposition enemies, then erase targets using the shared Space Gauge.
[b]KILLER QUEEN[/b] — Set bombs, choose when to detonate and use Sheer Heart Attack for automatic pressure.
[b]SILVER CHARIOT[/b] — Fast swordplay and Speed Gauge. Armor Off has its own cooldown and never blocks normal Summon/Dismiss.
[b]MAGICIAN'S RED[/b] — Cross Fire/Fire Trap, Flame Spray, Fire Control/Detector and Cross Fire Bomb for ranged fire and horde control.
[b]ECHOES ACT 3[/b] — Target-control Stand. Barrage helps set up THREE FREEZE, which holds a dangerous target for 9 seconds.

Full skill details, examples and conditional rules are available in the Wiki.

[h2]STAND LEVELS[/h2]

Each Stand has its own progression: [b]LV.1 → LV.100[/b]. Stand-caused zombie kills grant EXP. With Stand Levels enabled, LV.1 begins at [b]50% damage[/b] and LV.100 reaches [b]120% damage[/b]. Switching Stands preserves their separate progress.

[h2]CORE SYSTEMS[/h2]

• Directional First-Contact and real attack reach
• Stand combat, critical hits, Barrage Clash and Barrage Guard
• Time Stop / Time Erase with collision and state safeguards
• Stand Gauges, Tap / Hold / Full Charge abilities
• Stand Levels and multiplayer synchronization
• PvP / Safety and supported world / vehicle interactions
• Ongoing improvements to rear-hit knockback and environmental material drops

While a registered Stand is summoned, its owner takes [b]33% less NET damage[/b].
