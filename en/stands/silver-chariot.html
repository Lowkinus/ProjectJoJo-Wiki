SILVER CHARIOT

ROLE:
Hit & Run / Speed Gauge / Precision

Difficulty:
Medium–High

Best For:
Players who like constant movement, short attack windows and resource management built around speed.

PASSIVE — MOVEMENT + SPEED GAUGE

While summoned, Silver Chariot moves at about 1.12x speed.

Speed Gauge max: 100. Regen depends on movement:

  • Idle: +0.1/sec
  • Walking: +1/sec
  • Running/Jogging: +11/sec
  • Sprinting: +20/sec

Gauge regen pauses during external Time Stop or THREE FREEZE. Movement is part of the kit, not just positioning.

SKILL 1 — STAB

Cooldown: about 0.22 sec • Range: about 3.1 tiles • Full charge: about 1.1 sec.

Fast single-target pressure with no Speed Gauge cost.

SKILL 2 — RAPIER RUSH

Cooldown: about 3 sec • Cost: 18 Gauge • Normal: 18 hits • Full charge: 26 hits.

Your main close-range burst. Move to refill, spend it, then reposition.

SKILL 3 — CIRCLE SWEEP

Cooldown: about 3.5 sec • Cost: 25 Gauge • Sweep reach: about 4.2 tiles.

Full charge increases damage/impact, not the basic sweep footprint. Best when several targets are already around you.

SKILL 4 — RAPIER SHOT

Cooldown: about 5.2 sec • Range: about 8.2 tiles • Full charge: about 1.05 sec.

The sword follows the real shot line and can be intercepted by another body.

After firing, the sword takes roughly 5.2 sec to return. Silver Chariot skills stay locked during that return window, so fire it when you have room to disengage.
