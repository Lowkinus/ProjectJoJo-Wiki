BEGINNER GUIDE

Start here if you just installed the mod. Every section explains the rule first, then gives a concrete example.

BUILD 42+SP / HOST / MPCORE v0.23.0.280
01 • FIND AN ARROW

Find a Stand Arrow

Zombies have a base drop chance of about 1 in 250. Arrows can also appear in world containers, with garages/tool/mechanic storage being better places to search.

Example: if you are actively hunting for an Arrow, check garages and tool storage instead of opening only kitchen cupboards.
02 • USE THE ARROW

Random by default; Super Arrow can let you choose

One Arrow has 3 successful uses: 3/3 → 2/3 → 1/3 → gone. Normal worlds roll a random registered Stand. If the server enables Super Arrow, the Arrow opens a list of currently loaded Stands instead.

Example: if ATUM is not installed, Super Arrow cannot offer ATUM. It only lists Stands that really exist in that game session.
03 • BASIC CONTROLS

Summon first, then use Skills 1–4

Caps Lock summons/dismisses your Stand. Top-row 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 are Stand skills. Some skills change when held until FULL.

Example: The World Skill 4 is a quick Time Dash on tap, but becomes a 9-second Time Stop when fully charged.
04 • TARGETING

Choose how you want to aim

Options → Mods → Project JoJo lets you choose Mouse / Pointer, Facing Direction, or Controller Aim. The mode changes the direction source, not the hit rules.

Example: Facing Direction is useful with Project Viewpoint because you can fight where the character faces without depending on a visible mouse cursor.
05 • FIRST-CONTACT

The first body in the attack line gets hit

Long skills keep their real maximum range, but they do not magically select a target through another body or solid obstacle.

Example: if a zombie stands between you and another zombie, Star Finger may hit the front body first instead of skipping it.
06 • BARRAGE GUARD

Some Barrages can defend the front of the player

SP, TW, attack-mode Crazy Diamond, Silver Chariot and King Crimson can create Barrage Guard. Supported projectiles crossing that front line can be stopped.

Example: face a TW knife and Barrage at the right angle to defend; a knife from behind still hits.
07 • TIME STOP

Stopped time pauses people, damage and moving projectiles

Targets inside the stop cannot move. Damage is banked and applied when time resumes. Projectiles caught in the stopped area pause and continue from the same place afterward.

Example: throw a knife, stop time while it is flying, and the knife waits in the air until time moves again.
08 • LEVELS & DEFENSE

Every Stand levels separately

Each Stand has Lv.1–100 progression. Lv.1 starts at about 50% of the balanced level-scaled damage and Lv.100 reaches full power. While a registered Stand is summoned, the owner takes 33% less net damage.

Example: leveling Star Platinum does not delete your Crazy Diamond progress if you switch back later.
09 • HUD & AUDIO

Make the interface fit your screen

Client options include Normal / Minimal / Hidden HUD, HUD scale/opacity, personal Project JoJo volume, targeting mode and keybinds.

Example: Hidden HUD removes the passive Stand HUD, but required addon screens such as ATUM minigames and HARVEST Search still open.
10 • READ YOUR STAND PAGE

The four buttons can hide very different rules

The home page tells you the Stand style. The Stand page explains costs, charge behavior, special resources and examples.

Example: The Hand can look like a simple melee Stand until you understand that Pull, movement and ERASE all compete for the same Space Gauge.
