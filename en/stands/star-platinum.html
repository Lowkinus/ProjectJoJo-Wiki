STAR PLATINUM

ROLE:
Brawler / Crit Combo / Close Range / Time Control

Difficulty:
Medium

Best For:
Players who want aggressive melee, combos, high burst and Time Stop.

SKILL 1 — ORA PUNCH

Cooldown: approximately 0.36 sec
Range: approximately 2.75 tiles
Full Charge: approximately 1.2 sec

Star Platinum has a unique 5-stage Punch combo.

Keep punching within roughly:

0.95 seconds

to continue the combo chain.

The fifth Punch is the strongest hit.

Full charging the Punch dramatically increases its damage and impact.

HOW TO USE IT:

Normal Punches are excellent when something is already in your face.

Do not full-charge every single Punch.

Against one normal zombie:

Fast repeated punches are usually more practical.

Use the full charge when:

  • A dangerous target is approaching
  • You have distance
  • Time is stopped
  • You want a heavy opening hit
  • You simply need that zombie to reconsider its career

SKILL 2 — ORA BARRAGE

Cooldown: approximately 5 sec

Normal:
15 hits

Full Charge:
25 hits + increased damage

The Barrage continuously follows your aim.

It can participate in Barrage Clash.

TIP:

Against moving enemies, keep the mouse on the actual target direction.

The Stand has aim assistance, but Barrage is not a turret.

SKILL 3 — STAR FINGER

Cooldown: approximately 7 sec
Range: approximately 5 tiles
Full Charge: approximately 1.05 sec

Star Finger extends forward along the mouse direction.

It is a piercing line attack.

Enemies lined up along the finger's path can be hit instead of requiring the cursor to sit perfectly on one exact zombie.

BEST USE:

Line up several zombies in a corridor.

Congratulations.

You have invented zombie kebab.

SKILL 4 — TIME DASH / TIME STOP

Cooldown: approximately 35 sec

Tap:
Time Dash — approximately 4.5 tiles

Full Charge:
Time Stop

Wind-up after activation:
approximately 1.7 sec

Time Stop duration:
5 seconds

Time Dash is collision-aware.

It will not intentionally teleport you through solid walls, closed doors or invalid terrain.

TIP:

Do not think of Skill 4 as "the Time Stop button."

Half of its value is the Dash.

A 35-second Time Stop cooldown does NOT mean you should ignore a 4.5-tile emergency teleport.

STAR PLATINUM CRITICAL SYSTEM

Base Crit:
15%

Every Punch/Barrage contact that does NOT Crit:

+1 percentage point

Maximum:
50%

When Star Platinum finally Crits:

x3.5 damage

Then the Crit chance resets to 15%.

Star Finger can use the stored Crit chance.

However:

Star Finger does NOT build the Crit meter when it fails to Crit.

Punch and Barrage are your Crit builders.

PLAYSTYLE TIP:

If your Crit meter is high, consider saving it for:

  • A charged Punch
  • A heavy finisher
  • Star Finger
  • A Time Stop attack window

Do not waste a 48% Crit on the zombie already missing both arms unless that zombie personally offended you.
