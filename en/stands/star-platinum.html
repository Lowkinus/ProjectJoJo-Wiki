DifficultyMedium
Best forPlayers who want close-range pressure, resource-like crit management and a clutch Time Stop
Signature5-step combo + Crit
Core range2.75–3.1 tiles
Time Stop5 seconds

Skill 1 — ORA PUNCH

0.36s cooldown2.75 tiles1.2s full charge

Star Platinum is the one Stand in the current code with the true five-step punch chain. Keep hits inside the ~0.95s combo window and the fifth punch becomes the big finisher. A full hold trades speed for a heavier single hit.

CRITICAL SYSTEM

Starts at 15%Caps at 50%Critical x3.5

A real Punch or Barrage contact that does not crit adds +1 percentage point. When a crit lands, the meter resets to 15%. Star Finger can use the stored chance, but a non-crit Finger does not build it.

Skill 2 — ORA BARRAGE

5s cooldown3.1 tiles15 / 25 hits

Full charge takes 1.8s and raises the sequence to 25 hits. The Barrage steers with the mouse, builds the crit meter quickly, and creates Barrage Guard in front of you.

Skill 3 — STAR FINGER

7s cooldown5 tiles1.05s full charge

Still a piercing line attack. It can pass through multiple bodies on the same line, but the line follows your actual mouse direction and normal obstacles still matter. It can cash in your stored SP crit chance.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / TIME STOP

35s cooldownTap: 11.25-tile DashHold: 5s Stop

Tap for the long Time Dash. Full charge takes 1s, followed by roughly 1.7s of declaration/windup before the 5-second stop. Damage dealt to frozen targets is banked until time resumes.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Build pressure first

Do not spend Time Stop on the first thing that looks annoying. Punch and Barrage to build tempo — and Crit — then decide whether the finisher should be a heavy punch, Star Finger or the time button.

Close-range route

Punch chain → Barrage while the target is pinned → back to Punch. If the crowd starts closing, Time Dash out. Keeping a combo is not worth getting boxed in.

Crit discipline

A high crit meter is a loaded gun. Do not waste it on the zombie already falling over. Save it for a heavy target or a safer Star Finger finish.

Five seconds means move

Know the plan before you stop time: close distance, place damage, reposition. If you stop first and think second, half the window is already gone.

Keep this in mind: SP feels best when every hit is feeding the next decision. If you mash on cooldown and ignore Crit + Dash positioning, you are leaving half the Stand on the table.
