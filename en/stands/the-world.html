DifficultyMedium
Best forPlayers who want a straightforward bruiser with real range and the longest current Time Stop
SignatureHeavy burst + Knife
Knife range9 tiles
Time Stop9 seconds

Skill 1 — MUDA PUNCH

0.39s cooldown2.8 tiles1.2s full charge

A heavy single punch. The current combat code does not give The World Star Platinum’s five-step combo, so treat this as clean burst, not a chain you have to babysit.

Skill 2 — MUDA BARRAGE

5.2s cooldown3.1 tiles15 / 28 hits

Full charge takes 1.75s and raises it to 28 hits. It can Barrage Clash and it creates Barrage Guard.

Skill 3 — KNIFE THROW

1.6s cooldown9 tiles0.9s full charge

The knife flies on the real mouse line. The first body in the way can take it. Supported Barrage Guards can deflect it, so do not pretend it is free damage into a prepared opponent.

Skill 4 — TIME DASH / ZA WARUDO

42s cooldownTap: 13.125-tile DashHold: 9s Stop

Tap for a huge reposition. Full charge is 1s plus roughly 0.75s of windup, then nine seconds of stopped time.

HOW TO ACTUALLY PLAY IT

Knife before fists

Use Knife to force movement or thin the front, then Dash into Punch/Barrage. The World does not need to sprint straight into every problem.

Simple burst line

Knife → Time Dash in → Punch/Barrage. That is already a strong route without spending the 42-second ultimate.

Nine seconds is a budget

Use the long Stop when it changes the whole situation: a blocked escape, a giant crowd, a rescue, or multiple targets. Spending it on one harmless zombie is comedy, not strategy.

Time duel

If another Stop catches you, a fully charged TW Time Stop can create your own movement window. When your window ends, the older Stop can still take over again if it has time left.

Keep this in mind: The World has an answer for almost everything, but the 42-second Time Stop cooldown punishes using the biggest answer on the smallest problem.
