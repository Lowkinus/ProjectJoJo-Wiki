STAND ARROW

At default settings, zombies drop an Arrow at roughly 1 in 250. Arrows also enter a broad set of world loot containers.

  • Best places to search: garages, tool crates and mechanic storage.
  • General containers: wardrobes, desks, shelves, lockers and assorted junk containers can roll one too.
  • Vehicles: possible, but intentionally rarer than proper tool storage.

One Arrow has native Condition 3 and survives three successful Stand rolls. Condition is spent only after the reroll succeeds.

CONTROLS

CAPS LOCK — Summon / dismiss Stand
1 / 2 / 3 / 4 — Skills on the top number row

Several skills are dual-mode. Tap and full-hold are not just stronger/weaker versions — they can be different moves entirely. Time Dash becomes Time Stop. The Hand's dash becomes Pull. Killer Queen's First Bomb button becomes SHA on a full hold.

STAND LEVEL

Every Stand stores its own progression from Lv.1 to Lv.100. Lv.1 starts at about 50% of balanced damage and scales linearly to 100% at Lv.100.

  • A zombie kill caused by your Stand grants +1 EXP to that Stand.
  • Lv.1 needs 5 EXP; the requirement rises by +1 every four levels.
  • Rerolling does not delete another Stand's saved Level/EXP.
  • Rolling that Stand again restores its saved progression.
  • Character death resets all Stand progression for that character.
  • Disabling Stand Level in Sandbox makes every Stand behave as Lv.100.

DIRECTIONAL FIRST-CONTACT

Your mouse acts as a direction, not a guaranteed lock. Skills use their real maximum reach, then choose the first physical body intersecting the attack corridor.

  • Standing, downed and crawling targets use appropriate contact capsules.
  • A close cursor no longer shortens a long skill.
  • A body in front can take the hit before the target behind it.
  • Walls, doors, windows and vehicles still stop ordinary lines.
  • Area effects such as KQ Detonate and MR Cross Fire are intentional exceptions to straight-line contact.

BARRAGE CONTACT + CLASH

Barrages steer with the mouse while active. They do not permanently glue themselves to an old target. When a frame briefly finds nothing, the system allows about 240 ms of contact grace with 0.30 tile side tolerance.

Slow frames can catch up by up to 3 hits per update. A new real contact still takes priority immediately.

Barrage Clash

Supported offensive Barrages can collide when players are within roughly 5.25 tiles and actually face/aim into one another. Clash hits cancel against each other before normal damage. Crazy Diamond's Restore Rush is not an offensive Barrage and does not Clash.

BARRAGE GUARD

Guard is currently enabled for Star Platinum, The World, Crazy Diamond (Attack Barrage), Silver Chariot and King Crimson.

  • Total guard width is about 2.5 tiles.
  • The projectile must really cross the guard line from the front.
  • Direct support: TW Knife, SC Rapier Shot, CD Glass / Glass Homing.
  • It does not block explosions, area fire, area erasure or THREE FREEZE.
  • Crazy Diamond Restore Rush does not create Guard.
Vanilla firearms: a native per-hit interception hook exists, but live B42 validation across SP / Host / Dedicated is still required before firearm coverage is considered complete.
Third-party projectiles: mods must integrate JJS_BarrageGuard.interceptProjectile(attacker,sx,sy,ex,ey,z).

TIME STOP / TIME ERASE

Star Platinum: 5-second stop • The World: 9-second stop • King Crimson: 10-second Time Erase.

Time Stop freezes supported players, zombies, vehicles and movers. Damage dealt to frozen targets is banked and released when time resumes.

An SP/TW user caught in someone else's stopped time may fully charge their own Time Stop to earn their own finite movement window. When that personal window ends, any older Stop that still has time left takes over again.

Stop vs Erase

An active or pending Time Stop blocks a new full Time Erase. If Erase existed first, an incoming Stop spends its full duration as a counter budget against the remaining Erase time.

  • Stop shorter than Erase → Stop is consumed; Erase survives.
  • Equal → both cancel.
  • Stop longer → Erase ends; the excess becomes actual stopped time.

MULTIPLAYER / PVP / SAFETY

Stand HP damage still obeys Project Zomboid PvP/Safety. The mod does not sneak damage around a protected player.

Non-damaging utility and CC can follow separate rules: THREE FREEZE, Space Pull and Epitaph reposition are mechanics, not ordinary HP hits. First Bomb is an offensive setup, so protected players are ignored by its aim selection instead of stealing the mark from your intended target.
