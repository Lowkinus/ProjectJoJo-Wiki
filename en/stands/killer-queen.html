KILLER QUEEN

ROLE:
Trap / AOE / Autonomous Pressure

Difficulty:
High

Best For:
Players who enjoy preparation, controlling space and letting a tiny angry tank make its own poor decisions.

SKILL 1 — PUNCH

Cooldown:
approximately 0.39 sec

Killer Queen is not primarily a melee Stand.

The Punch exists so you are not completely helpless when something reaches you.

Use bombs to create the advantage first.

SKILL 2 — BARRAGE

Cooldown:
approximately 5.2 sec

Normal:
16 hits

Full:
24 hits

Useful for close pressure and Barrage Clash.

Still not the main reason you picked Killer Queen.

You picked Killer Queen because you have trust issues with normal objects.

SKILL 3 TAP — FIRST BOMB

Tap Skill 3:

Place / Attach First Bomb

Range:
approximately 5.5 tiles

Cooldown:
approximately 0.7 sec

If the system finds a living target under your aim:

The bomb attaches to that target and follows it.

Otherwise:

The bomb remains at the selected world position.

Press Skill 3 again:

Retrieve / remove the placed bomb.

Retrieving the bomb is allowed even if the original placement cooldown has not fully finished.

USE CASES:

Attach it to a zombie entering a group.

Let it walk into its friends.

Detonate.

Teamwork.

The zombie did not consent to being the delivery vehicle.

SKILL 4 — DETONATE

Cooldown:
approximately 6.8 sec

Base blast radius:
approximately 2.5 tiles

Full charge increases both damage and blast radius.

Full blast radius is approximately:

3.25 tiles

Ground bombs use distance falloff:

Targets near the center take much more damage than targets near the outer edge.

If First Bomb is attached directly to a living target:

The marked target receives the primary hit.

Nearby targets receive reduced splash.

TIP:

Ground bomb:
Best when you can predict where a horde will move.

Attached bomb:
Best when you can turn one zombie into Amazon Prime.

SKILL 3 FULL HOLD — SHEER HEART ATTACK

Full holding Skill 3 does NOT place First Bomb.

It toggles:

SHEER HEART ATTACK

SHA automatically:

  • Searches for valid targets
  • Moves toward them
  • Enters attack range
  • Plays its bomb wind-up
  • Explodes
  • Retreats
  • Finds another target
  • Continues operating without constant player input

Search range:
approximately 28 tiles

Attack distance:
approximately 2.65 tiles

Bomb wind-up:
approximately 1.5 sec

Attack cooldown:
approximately 4 sec

SHA explosion damage:

50% of Killer Queen's main Detonate base damage

This makes SHA meaningful autonomous pressure while still weaker per explosion than KQ's main Detonate.

SHA DURABILITY / GUARD MODE

Maximum HP:

150

SHA no longer loses HP simply because it is deployed.

If it is standing guard and does not explode or get attacked:

Its HP does not drain over time.

That means you can leave it guarding a house / area without paying an idle HP tax.

Its attack activity has a cost instead:

Every 2 real SHA explosions = -1 HP

Explosion 1 = no HP cost
Explosion 2 = -1 HP
Explosion 3 = no HP cost
Explosion 4 = -1 HP
...and so on.

Recalling and redeploying SHA does NOT reset that two-explosion progress.

While stored:

+1 HP every 5 seconds

Incoming damage to SHA still reduces its HP normally.

If SHA reaches 0 HP:

  • SHA is destroyed
  • Your right hand is injured
  • Deep wound / fracture / bleeding / pain can be applied
  • SHA cannot be redeployed until its repair gauge reaches full again

After a destroyed SHA fully repairs, the two-explosion counter starts fresh.

PVP NOTE:

PvP / Safety-protected players are not valid autonomous SHA targets.
