PLAYSTYLE • Single-target control • THREE FREEZE

ECHOES ACT 3

Echoes Act 3 is a control Stand, not a damage race. Pick one target, establish contact, then lock it in place for 9 seconds so you or your team can act safely.

Current guide refresh • Project JoJo Core
DifficultyEasy–Medium
Freeze duration9 sec
Freeze range9 tiles
Best atStopping one threat

Skill 1 — BARRAGE + MARK

CD 4.7s • Range 2.8 • 14 / 23 hits

Barrage deals light damage and records the real target you were hitting. At the end of the Rush the system can carry that target into the THREE FREEZE setup instead of losing it.

Example: Use the Rush on the specific zombie you need disabled, not randomly into a crowd where you do not care which target gets selected.

Skill 2 — THREE FREEZE

CD 9s • Range 9 • Duration 9s

Locks one valid target. The victim falls once, then movement is blocked for 9 seconds. The Echoes owner is protected from being locked by their own THREE FREEZE.

Example: Freeze the strongest zombie in a group, kill the weaker ones first, then return to the frozen threat before the 9 seconds end.

Vehicles

Vehicle movement can also be held by the EA3 freeze path

The freeze system has a vehicle path as well, using the shared vehicle-freeze safety instead of treating a car like a normal living body.

Example: Use the skill knowing that the mod cleans up the lock when the effect ends, the owner changes Stand, dies or loses the state.

HOW TO THINK ABOUT THIS STAND

Do not memorize jargon

Focus on the loop: what builds your resource, what spends it, and which skill solves the problem in front of you.

Use the examples

The examples above are not fixed combos. They are situations that explain why the mechanic exists, so you can adapt it to your own fight.
